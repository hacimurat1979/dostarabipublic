74 The Cloaked One

Meccan · 56 verses · juz 29 · 2 ruku · revelation order 4

The Arabic text comes from Tanzil and the translations from QuranEnc, unchanged. A translation is not the Qur'an itself; the Arabic text is a separate block. This page is not a commentary or interpretation. Translation shown: Uluslararası Nur Merkezi

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلْمُدَّثِّرُ ﴿١﴾

1O you who covers himself [with a garment],[1788]

[1788] Referring to the Prophet (ﷺ).
Suggest a correction

قُمْ فَأَنذِرْ ﴿٢﴾

2Arise and warn

Suggest a correction

وَرَبَّكَ فَكَبِّرْ ﴿٣﴾

3And your Lord glorify

Suggest a correction

وَثِيَابَكَ فَطَهِّرْ ﴿٤﴾

4And your clothing purify

Suggest a correction

وَٱلرُّجْزَ فَٱهْجُرْ ﴿٥﴾

5And uncleanliness[1789] avoid

[1789] Specifically, idols or generally, bad conduct and morals.
Suggest a correction

وَلَا تَمْنُن تَسْتَكْثِرُ ﴿٦﴾

6And do not confer favor to acquire more[1790]

[1790] An alternative meaning is "Do not consider any favor you have conferred to be great."
Suggest a correction

وَلِرَبِّكَ فَٱصْبِرْ ﴿٧﴾

7But for your Lord be patient.

Suggest a correction

فَإِذَا نُقِرَ فِى ٱلنَّاقُورِ ﴿٨﴾

8And when the trumpet is blown,

Suggest a correction

فَذَٰلِكَ يَوْمَئِذٍ يَوْمٌ عَسِيرٌ ﴿٩﴾

9That Day will be a difficult day

Suggest a correction

عَلَى ٱلْكَـٰفِرِينَ غَيْرُ يَسِيرٍ ﴿١٠﴾

10For the disbelievers - not easy.

Suggest a correction

ذَرْنِى وَمَنْ خَلَقْتُ وَحِيدًا ﴿١١﴾

11Leave Me with the one I created alone[1791]

[1791] i.e., without wealth or children. The reference is to al-Waleed bin al-Mugheerah, who after inclining toward the Qur’ān, denied it publicly in order to win the approval of the Quraysh.
Suggest a correction

وَجَعَلْتُ لَهُۥ مَالًا مَّمْدُودًا ﴿١٢﴾

12And to whom I granted extensive wealth

Suggest a correction

وَبَنِينَ شُهُودًا ﴿١٣﴾

13And children present [with him]

Suggest a correction

وَمَهَّدتُّ لَهُۥ تَمْهِيدًا ﴿١٤﴾

14And spread [everything] before him, easing [his life].

Suggest a correction

ثُمَّ يَطْمَعُ أَنْ أَزِيدَ ﴿١٥﴾

15Then he desires that I should add more.

Suggest a correction

كَلَّآ ۖ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ لِـَٔايَـٰتِنَا عَنِيدًا ﴿١٦﴾

16No! Indeed, he has been toward Our verses obstinate.

Suggest a correction

سَأُرْهِقُهُۥ صَعُودًا ﴿١٧﴾

17I will cover him with arduous torment.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّهُۥ فَكَّرَ وَقَدَّرَ ﴿١٨﴾

18Indeed, he thought and deliberated.[1792]

[1792] About what he would say concerning the Qur’ān and how he might discredit the Prophet (ﷺ).
Suggest a correction

فَقُتِلَ كَيْفَ قَدَّرَ ﴿١٩﴾

19So may he be destroyed [for] how he deliberated.

Suggest a correction

ثُمَّ قُتِلَ كَيْفَ قَدَّرَ ﴿٢٠﴾

20Then may he be destroyed [for] how he deliberated.

Suggest a correction

ثُمَّ نَظَرَ ﴿٢١﴾

21Then he considered [again];

Suggest a correction

ثُمَّ عَبَسَ وَبَسَرَ ﴿٢٢﴾

22Then he frowned and scowled;

Suggest a correction

ثُمَّ أَدْبَرَ وَٱسْتَكْبَرَ ﴿٢٣﴾

23Then he turned back and was arrogant

Suggest a correction

فَقَالَ إِنْ هَـٰذَآ إِلَّا سِحْرٌ يُؤْثَرُ ﴿٢٤﴾

24And said, "This is not but magic imitated [from others].

Suggest a correction

إِنْ هَـٰذَآ إِلَّا قَوْلُ ٱلْبَشَرِ ﴿٢٥﴾

25This is not but the word of a human being."

Suggest a correction

سَأُصْلِيهِ سَقَرَ ﴿٢٦﴾

26I will drive him into Saqar.[1793]

[1793] One of the proper names of Hell.
Suggest a correction

وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا سَقَرُ ﴿٢٧﴾

27And what can make you know what is Saqar?

Suggest a correction

لَا تُبْقِى وَلَا تَذَرُ ﴿٢٨﴾

28It lets nothing remain and leaves nothing [unburned],

Suggest a correction

لَوَّاحَةٌ لِّلْبَشَرِ ﴿٢٩﴾

29Altering [i.e., blackening] the skins.

Suggest a correction

عَلَيْهَا تِسْعَةَ عَشَرَ ﴿٣٠﴾

30Over it are nineteen [angels].

Suggest a correction

وَمَا جَعَلْنَآ أَصْحَـٰبَ ٱلنَّارِ إِلَّا مَلَـٰٓئِكَةً ۙ وَمَا جَعَلْنَا عِدَّتَهُمْ إِلَّا فِتْنَةً لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ لِيَسْتَيْقِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُوا۟ ٱلْكِتَـٰبَ وَيَزْدَادَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓا۟ إِيمَـٰنًا ۙ وَلَا يَرْتَابَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُوا۟ ٱلْكِتَـٰبَ وَٱلْمُؤْمِنُونَ ۙ وَلِيَقُولَ ٱلَّذِينَ فِى قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضٌ وَٱلْكَـٰفِرُونَ مَاذَآ أَرَادَ ٱللَّهُ بِهَـٰذَا مَثَلًا ۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يُضِلُّ ٱللَّهُ مَن يَشَآءُ وَيَهْدِى مَن يَشَآءُ ۚ وَمَا يَعْلَمُ جُنُودَ رَبِّكَ إِلَّا هُوَ ۚ وَمَا هِىَ إِلَّا ذِكْرَىٰ لِلْبَشَرِ ﴿٣١﴾

31And We have not made the keepers of the Fire except angels. And We have not made their number except as a trial for those who disbelieve - that those who were given the Scripture will be convinced and those who have believed will increase in faith and those who were given the Scripture and the believers will not doubt and that those in whose hearts is disease [i.e., hypocrisy] and the disbelievers will say, "What does Allāh intend by this as an example?" Thus does Allāh send astray whom He wills and guide whom He wills. And none knows the soldiers of your Lord except Him. And it [i.e., mention of the Fire] is not but a reminder to humanity.

Suggest a correction

كَلَّا وَٱلْقَمَرِ ﴿٣٢﴾

32No! By the moon

Suggest a correction

وَٱلَّيْلِ إِذْ أَدْبَرَ ﴿٣٣﴾

33And [by] the night when it departs

Suggest a correction

وَٱلصُّبْحِ إِذَآ أَسْفَرَ ﴿٣٤﴾

34And [by] the morning when it brightens,

Suggest a correction

إِنَّهَا لَإِحْدَى ٱلْكُبَرِ ﴿٣٥﴾

35Indeed, it [i.e., the Fire] is of the greatest [afflictions]

Suggest a correction

نَذِيرًا لِّلْبَشَرِ ﴿٣٦﴾

36As a warning to humanity -

Suggest a correction

لِمَن شَآءَ مِنكُمْ أَن يَتَقَدَّمَ أَوْ يَتَأَخَّرَ ﴿٣٧﴾

37To whoever wills among you to proceed[1794] or stay behind.

[1794] To righteousness by acceptance of the warning.
Suggest a correction

كُلُّ نَفْسٍۭ بِمَا كَسَبَتْ رَهِينَةٌ ﴿٣٨﴾

38Every soul, for what it has earned, will be retained[1795]

[1795] i.e., subject or held responsible.
Cited in Dost Arabî 1
  • The Religion Is Your Act — the Law Is His Fusus · direct citation
Suggest a correction

إِلَّآ أَصْحَـٰبَ ٱلْيَمِينِ ﴿٣٩﴾

39Except the companions of the right,[1796].

[1796] i.e., the righteous who receive their records in their right hands.
Suggest a correction

فِى جَنَّـٰتٍ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ ﴿٤٠﴾

40[Who will be] in gardens, questioning each other

Suggest a correction

عَنِ ٱلْمُجْرِمِينَ ﴿٤١﴾

41About the criminals,

Suggest a correction

مَا سَلَكَكُمْ فِى سَقَرَ ﴿٤٢﴾

42[And asking them], "What put you into Saqar?"

Suggest a correction

قَالُوا۟ لَمْ نَكُ مِنَ ٱلْمُصَلِّينَ ﴿٤٣﴾

43They will say, "We were not of those who prayed,

Suggest a correction

وَلَمْ نَكُ نُطْعِمُ ٱلْمِسْكِينَ ﴿٤٤﴾

44Nor did we used to feed the poor.

Suggest a correction

وَكُنَّا نَخُوضُ مَعَ ٱلْخَآئِضِينَ ﴿٤٥﴾

45And we used to enter into vain discourse with those who engaged [in it],

Suggest a correction

وَكُنَّا نُكَذِّبُ بِيَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ ﴿٤٦﴾

46And we used to deny the Day of Recompense

Suggest a correction

حَتَّىٰٓ أَتَىٰنَا ٱلْيَقِينُ ﴿٤٧﴾

47Until there came to us the certainty [i.e., death]."

Suggest a correction

فَمَا تَنفَعُهُمْ شَفَـٰعَةُ ٱلشَّـٰفِعِينَ ﴿٤٨﴾

48So there will not benefit them the intercession of [any] intercessors.

Suggest a correction

فَمَا لَهُمْ عَنِ ٱلتَّذْكِرَةِ مُعْرِضِينَ ﴿٤٩﴾

49Then what is [the matter] with them that they are, from the reminder, turning away

Suggest a correction

كَأَنَّهُمْ حُمُرٌ مُّسْتَنفِرَةٌ ﴿٥٠﴾

50As if they were alarmed donkeys

Suggest a correction

فَرَّتْ مِن قَسْوَرَةٍۭ ﴿٥١﴾

51Fleeing from a lion?

Suggest a correction

بَلْ يُرِيدُ كُلُّ ٱمْرِئٍ مِّنْهُمْ أَن يُؤْتَىٰ صُحُفًا مُّنَشَّرَةً ﴿٥٢﴾

52Rather, every person among them desires that he[1797] would be given scriptures spread about.[1798]

[1797] Instead of Muḥammad (ﷺ). [1798] i.e., made public. Much of their refusal of his message was due to envy and jealousy of the Prophet (ﷺ).
Suggest a correction

كَلَّا ۖ بَل لَّا يَخَافُونَ ٱلْـَٔاخِرَةَ ﴿٥٣﴾

53No! But they do not fear the Hereafter.

Suggest a correction

كَلَّآ إِنَّهُۥ تَذْكِرَةٌ ﴿٥٤﴾

54No! Indeed, it [i.e., the Qur’ān] is a reminder

Suggest a correction

فَمَن شَآءَ ذَكَرَهُۥ ﴿٥٥﴾

55Then whoever wills will remember it.

Suggest a correction

وَمَا يَذْكُرُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُ ۚ هُوَ أَهْلُ ٱلتَّقْوَىٰ وَأَهْلُ ٱلْمَغْفِرَةِ ﴿٥٦﴾

56And they will not remember except that Allāh wills. He is worthy of fear and adequate for [granting] forgiveness.

Suggest a correction
