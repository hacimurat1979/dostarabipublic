Physics papers: timeline

This section is not a reading summary. It is an attempt to read Dost's words side by side with the findings of physics that do not fit reason; it is our own reading frame. We do not say “the same” or “confirmed”: we only ask, and we say where they part ways. The text is our own rendering of a Turkish original.

The papers of each topic, by year of publication. Each row is a group, each dot a paper; touch a dot to see its name, click it to go to its summary. In this chart there are no citation arrows between papers: only time. Saying which rests on which would require checking every citation in its source; that has not been done.
