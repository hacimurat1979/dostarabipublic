54 The Moon

Meccan · 55 verses · juz 27 · 3 ruku · revelation order 37

The Arabic text comes from Tanzil and the translations from QuranEnc, unchanged. A translation is not the Qur'an itself; the Arabic text is a separate block. This page is not a commentary or interpretation. Translation shown: Uluslararası Nur Merkezi

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

ٱقْتَرَبَتِ ٱلسَّاعَةُ وَٱنشَقَّ ٱلْقَمَرُ ﴿١﴾

1The Hour has come near, and the moon has split [in two].[1577]

[1577] This was a sign given by Allāh to Prophet Muḥammad (ﷺ) when the Quraysh challenged him to show them a miracle.
Suggest a correction

وَإِن يَرَوْا۟ ءَايَةً يُعْرِضُوا۟ وَيَقُولُوا۟ سِحْرٌ مُّسْتَمِرٌّ ﴿٢﴾

2And if they see a sign [i.e., miracle], they turn away and say, "Passing magic."[1578]

[1578] Or "Continuing magic."
Cited in Dost Arabî 1
  • Power Is Incidental — Origin Is Weakness Fusus · direct citation
Suggest a correction

وَكَذَّبُوا۟ وَٱتَّبَعُوٓا۟ أَهْوَآءَهُمْ ۚ وَكُلُّ أَمْرٍ مُّسْتَقِرٌّ ﴿٣﴾

3And they denied and followed their inclinations. But for every matter is a [time of] settlement.

Suggest a correction

وَلَقَدْ جَآءَهُم مِّنَ ٱلْأَنۢبَآءِ مَا فِيهِ مُزْدَجَرٌ ﴿٤﴾

4And there has already come to them of information that in which there is deterrence.

Suggest a correction

حِكْمَةٌۢ بَـٰلِغَةٌ ۖ فَمَا تُغْنِ ٱلنُّذُرُ ﴿٥﴾

5Extensive wisdom - but warning does not avail [them].

Suggest a correction

فَتَوَلَّ عَنْهُمْ ۘ يَوْمَ يَدْعُ ٱلدَّاعِ إِلَىٰ شَىْءٍ نُّكُرٍ ﴿٦﴾

6So leave them, [O Muḥammad]. The Day the Caller[1579] calls to something forbidding,

[1579] Said to be an angel announcing the account and judgement.
Suggest a correction

خُشَّعًا أَبْصَـٰرُهُمْ يَخْرُجُونَ مِنَ ٱلْأَجْدَاثِ كَأَنَّهُمْ جَرَادٌ مُّنتَشِرٌ ﴿٧﴾

7Their eyes humbled, they will emerge from the graves as if they were locusts spreading,

Suggest a correction

مُّهْطِعِينَ إِلَى ٱلدَّاعِ ۖ يَقُولُ ٱلْكَـٰفِرُونَ هَـٰذَا يَوْمٌ عَسِرٌ ﴿٨﴾

8Racing ahead toward the Caller. The disbelievers will say, "This is a difficult Day."

Suggest a correction

كَذَّبَتْ قَبْلَهُمْ قَوْمُ نُوحٍ فَكَذَّبُوا۟ عَبْدَنَا وَقَالُوا۟ مَجْنُونٌ وَٱزْدُجِرَ ﴿٩﴾

9The people of Noah denied before them, and they denied Our servant and said, "A madman," and he was repelled.

Suggest a correction

فَدَعَا رَبَّهُۥٓ أَنِّى مَغْلُوبٌ فَٱنتَصِرْ ﴿١٠﴾

10So he invoked his Lord, "Indeed, I am overpowered, so help."

Suggest a correction

فَفَتَحْنَآ أَبْوَٰبَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ بِمَآءٍ مُّنْهَمِرٍ ﴿١١﴾

11Then We opened the gates of the heaven with rain pouring down

Suggest a correction

وَفَجَّرْنَا ٱلْأَرْضَ عُيُونًا فَٱلْتَقَى ٱلْمَآءُ عَلَىٰٓ أَمْرٍ قَدْ قُدِرَ ﴿١٢﴾

12And caused the earth to burst with springs, and the waters met for a matter already predestined.

Suggest a correction

وَحَمَلْنَـٰهُ عَلَىٰ ذَاتِ أَلْوَٰحٍ وَدُسُرٍ ﴿١٣﴾

13And We carried him on a [construction of] planks and nails,

Suggest a correction

تَجْرِى بِأَعْيُنِنَا جَزَآءً لِّمَن كَانَ كُفِرَ ﴿١٤﴾

14Sailing under Our observation as reward for he who had been denied.

Suggest a correction

وَلَقَد تَّرَكْنَـٰهَآ ءَايَةً فَهَلْ مِن مُّدَّكِرٍ ﴿١٥﴾

15And We left it as a sign, so is there any who will remember?

Suggest a correction

فَكَيْفَ كَانَ عَذَابِى وَنُذُرِ ﴿١٦﴾

16And how [severe] were My punishment and warning.[1580]

[1580] To those after them, who were expected to derive a lesson from previous occurrences.
Suggest a correction

وَلَقَدْ يَسَّرْنَا ٱلْقُرْءَانَ لِلذِّكْرِ فَهَلْ مِن مُّدَّكِرٍ ﴿١٧﴾

17And We have certainly made the Qur’ān easy for remembrance, so is there any who will remember?

Suggest a correction

كَذَّبَتْ عَادٌ فَكَيْفَ كَانَ عَذَابِى وَنُذُرِ ﴿١٨﴾

18ʿAad denied; and how [severe] were My punishment and warning.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّآ أَرْسَلْنَا عَلَيْهِمْ رِيحًا صَرْصَرًا فِى يَوْمِ نَحْسٍ مُّسْتَمِرٍّ ﴿١٩﴾

19Indeed, We sent upon them a screaming wind on a day of continuous misfortune,

Suggest a correction

تَنزِعُ ٱلنَّاسَ كَأَنَّهُمْ أَعْجَازُ نَخْلٍ مُّنقَعِرٍ ﴿٢٠﴾

20Extracting the people[1581] as if they were trunks of palm trees uprooted.

[1581] From their hiding places.
Suggest a correction

فَكَيْفَ كَانَ عَذَابِى وَنُذُرِ ﴿٢١﴾

21And how [severe] were My punishment and warning.

Suggest a correction

وَلَقَدْ يَسَّرْنَا ٱلْقُرْءَانَ لِلذِّكْرِ فَهَلْ مِن مُّدَّكِرٍ ﴿٢٢﴾

22And We have certainly made the Qur’ān easy for remembrance, so is there any who will remember?

Suggest a correction

كَذَّبَتْ ثَمُودُ بِٱلنُّذُرِ ﴿٢٣﴾

23Thamūd denied the warning.

Suggest a correction

فَقَالُوٓا۟ أَبَشَرًا مِّنَّا وَٰحِدًا نَّتَّبِعُهُۥٓ إِنَّآ إِذًا لَّفِى ضَلَـٰلٍ وَسُعُرٍ ﴿٢٤﴾

24And said, "Is it one human being[1582] among us that we should follow? Indeed, we would then be in error and madness.

[1582] i.e., the prophet Ṣāliḥ.
Suggest a correction

أَءُلْقِىَ ٱلذِّكْرُ عَلَيْهِ مِنۢ بَيْنِنَا بَلْ هُوَ كَذَّابٌ أَشِرٌ ﴿٢٥﴾

25Has the message been sent down upon him from among us? Rather, he is an insolent liar."

Suggest a correction

سَيَعْلَمُونَ غَدًا مَّنِ ٱلْكَذَّابُ ٱلْأَشِرُ ﴿٢٦﴾

26They will know tomorrow who is the insolent liar.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّا مُرْسِلُوا۟ ٱلنَّاقَةِ فِتْنَةً لَّهُمْ فَٱرْتَقِبْهُمْ وَٱصْطَبِرْ ﴿٢٧﴾

27Indeed, We are sending the she-camel as trial for them, so watch them and be patient.[1583]

[1583] This and the following verse are an address to Ṣāliḥ (upon him be peace).
Suggest a correction

وَنَبِّئْهُمْ أَنَّ ٱلْمَآءَ قِسْمَةٌۢ بَيْنَهُمْ ۖ كُلُّ شِرْبٍ مُّحْتَضَرٌ ﴿٢٨﴾

28And inform them that the water is shared between them,[1584] each [day of] drink attended [by turn].

[1584] i.e., between the tribe of Thamūd and the she-camel - a day for each to drink.
Suggest a correction

فَنَادَوْا۟ صَاحِبَهُمْ فَتَعَاطَىٰ فَعَقَرَ ﴿٢٩﴾

29But they called their companion,[1585] and he dared[1586] and hamstrung [her].

[1585] i.e., the worst and most despicable among them. [1586] Or "he took," referring to his sword or to the she-camel.
Suggest a correction

فَكَيْفَ كَانَ عَذَابِى وَنُذُرِ ﴿٣٠﴾

30And how [severe] were My punishment and warning.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّآ أَرْسَلْنَا عَلَيْهِمْ صَيْحَةً وَٰحِدَةً فَكَانُوا۟ كَهَشِيمِ ٱلْمُحْتَظِرِ ﴿٣١﴾

31Indeed, We sent upon them one shriek [i.e., blast from the sky], and they became like the dry twig fragments of an [animal] pen.

Suggest a correction

وَلَقَدْ يَسَّرْنَا ٱلْقُرْءَانَ لِلذِّكْرِ فَهَلْ مِن مُّدَّكِرٍ ﴿٣٢﴾

32And We have certainly made the Qur’ān easy for remembrance, so is there any who will remember?

Suggest a correction

كَذَّبَتْ قَوْمُ لُوطٍۭ بِٱلنُّذُرِ ﴿٣٣﴾

33The people of Lot denied the warning.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّآ أَرْسَلْنَا عَلَيْهِمْ حَاصِبًا إِلَّآ ءَالَ لُوطٍ ۖ نَّجَّيْنَـٰهُم بِسَحَرٍ ﴿٣٤﴾

34Indeed, We sent upon them a storm of stones, except the family of Lot - We saved them before dawn.

Suggest a correction

نِّعْمَةً مِّنْ عِندِنَا ۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نَجْزِى مَن شَكَرَ ﴿٣٥﴾

35As favor from Us. Thus do We reward he who is grateful.

Suggest a correction

وَلَقَدْ أَنذَرَهُم بَطْشَتَنَا فَتَمَارَوْا۟ بِٱلنُّذُرِ ﴿٣٦﴾

36And he had already warned them of Our assault, but they disputed the warning.

Suggest a correction

وَلَقَدْ رَٰوَدُوهُ عَن ضَيْفِهِۦ فَطَمَسْنَآ أَعْيُنَهُمْ فَذُوقُوا۟ عَذَابِى وَنُذُرِ ﴿٣٧﴾

37And they had demanded from him his guests, but We obliterated their eyes, [saying], "Taste My punishment and warning."

Suggest a correction

وَلَقَدْ صَبَّحَهُم بُكْرَةً عَذَابٌ مُّسْتَقِرٌّ ﴿٣٨﴾

38And there came upon them by morning an abiding punishment.

Suggest a correction

فَذُوقُوا۟ عَذَابِى وَنُذُرِ ﴿٣٩﴾

39So taste My punishment and warning.

Suggest a correction

وَلَقَدْ يَسَّرْنَا ٱلْقُرْءَانَ لِلذِّكْرِ فَهَلْ مِن مُّدَّكِرٍ ﴿٤٠﴾

40And We have certainly made the Qur’ān easy for remembrance, so is there any who will remember?

Suggest a correction

وَلَقَدْ جَآءَ ءَالَ فِرْعَوْنَ ٱلنُّذُرُ ﴿٤١﴾

41And there certainly came to the people of Pharaoh warning.

Suggest a correction

كَذَّبُوا۟ بِـَٔايَـٰتِنَا كُلِّهَا فَأَخَذْنَـٰهُمْ أَخْذَ عَزِيزٍ مُّقْتَدِرٍ ﴿٤٢﴾

42They denied Our signs, all of them, so We seized them with a seizure of one Exalted in Might and Perfect in Ability.

Suggest a correction

أَكُفَّارُكُمْ خَيْرٌ مِّنْ أُو۟لَـٰٓئِكُمْ أَمْ لَكُم بَرَآءَةٌ فِى ٱلزُّبُرِ ﴿٤٣﴾

43Are your disbelievers better than those [former ones], or have you immunity in the scriptures?

Suggest a correction

أَمْ يَقُولُونَ نَحْنُ جَمِيعٌ مُّنتَصِرٌ ﴿٤٤﴾

44Or do they say, "We are an assembly supporting [each other]"?

Suggest a correction

سَيُهْزَمُ ٱلْجَمْعُ وَيُوَلُّونَ ٱلدُّبُرَ ﴿٤٥﴾

45[Their] assembly will be defeated, and they will turn their backs [in retreat].[1587]

[1587] This foretold event took place on the day of Badr.
Suggest a correction

بَلِ ٱلسَّاعَةُ مَوْعِدُهُمْ وَٱلسَّاعَةُ أَدْهَىٰ وَأَمَرُّ ﴿٤٦﴾

46But the Hour is their appointment [for due punishment], and the Hour is more disastrous and more bitter.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّ ٱلْمُجْرِمِينَ فِى ضَلَـٰلٍ وَسُعُرٍ ﴿٤٧﴾

47Indeed, the criminals are in error and madness.[1588].

[1588] Or "in blazing fires."
Suggest a correction

يَوْمَ يُسْحَبُونَ فِى ٱلنَّارِ عَلَىٰ وُجُوهِهِمْ ذُوقُوا۟ مَسَّ سَقَرَ ﴿٤٨﴾

48The Day they are dragged into the Fire on their faces [it will be said], "Taste the touch of Saqar."[1589]

[1589] One of the proper names of Hell.
Suggest a correction

إِنَّا كُلَّ شَىْءٍ خَلَقْنَـٰهُ بِقَدَرٍ ﴿٤٩﴾

49Indeed, all things We created with predestination.

Suggest a correction

وَمَآ أَمْرُنَآ إِلَّا وَٰحِدَةٌ كَلَمْحٍۭ بِٱلْبَصَرِ ﴿٥٠﴾

50And Our command is but one, like a glance of the eye.

Cited in Dost Arabî 1
  • Rühullah — Born of the Divine Breath Fusus · direct citation
Suggest a correction

وَلَقَدْ أَهْلَكْنَآ أَشْيَاعَكُمْ فَهَلْ مِن مُّدَّكِرٍ ﴿٥١﴾

51And We have already destroyed your kinds,[1590] so is there any who will remember?

[1590] i.e., those similar to you in attitude and behavior when they rejected Allāh's messengers.
Suggest a correction

وَكُلُّ شَىْءٍ فَعَلُوهُ فِى ٱلزُّبُرِ ﴿٥٢﴾

52And everything they did is in written records.

Suggest a correction

وَكُلُّ صَغِيرٍ وَكَبِيرٍ مُّسْتَطَرٌ ﴿٥٣﴾

53And every small and great [thing] is inscribed.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّ ٱلْمُتَّقِينَ فِى جَنَّـٰتٍ وَنَهَرٍ ﴿٥٤﴾

54Indeed, the righteous will be among gardens and rivers,

Suggest a correction

فِى مَقْعَدِ صِدْقٍ عِندَ مَلِيكٍ مُّقْتَدِرٍۭ ﴿٥٥﴾

55In a seat of honor near a Sovereign, Perfect in Ability.[1591]

[1591] Who accomplishes whatever He wills whenever He wills.
