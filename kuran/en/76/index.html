الانسان

76 The Man

Medinan · 31 verses · juz 29 · 2 ruku · revelation order 98

The Arabic text comes from Tanzil and the translations from QuranEnc, unchanged. A translation is not the Qur'an itself; the Arabic text is a separate block. This page is not a commentary or interpretation. Translation shown: Uluslararası Nur Merkezi

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

هَلْ أَتَىٰ عَلَى ٱلْإِنسَـٰنِ حِينٌ مِّنَ ٱلدَّهْرِ لَمْ يَكُن شَيْـًٔا مَّذْكُورًا ﴿١﴾

1Has there [not] come upon man a period of time when he was not a thing [even] mentioned?

Cited in Dost Arabî 1
  • The Word of Luqman Fusus · direct citation
Suggest a correction

إِنَّا خَلَقْنَا ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ مِن نُّطْفَةٍ أَمْشَاجٍ نَّبْتَلِيهِ فَجَعَلْنَـٰهُ سَمِيعًۢا بَصِيرًا ﴿٢﴾

2Indeed, We created man from a sperm-drop mixture[1811] that We may try him; and We made him hearing and seeing.

[1811] i.e., a combination of the male and female substance, within the womb.
Suggest a correction

إِنَّا هَدَيْنَـٰهُ ٱلسَّبِيلَ إِمَّا شَاكِرًا وَإِمَّا كَفُورًا ﴿٣﴾

3Indeed, We guided him to the way, be he grateful or be he ungrateful.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّآ أَعْتَدْنَا لِلْكَـٰفِرِينَ سَلَـٰسِلَا۟ وَأَغْلَـٰلًا وَسَعِيرًا ﴿٤﴾

4Indeed, We have prepared for the disbelievers chains and shackles and a blaze.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّ ٱلْأَبْرَارَ يَشْرَبُونَ مِن كَأْسٍ كَانَ مِزَاجُهَا كَافُورًا ﴿٥﴾

5Indeed, the righteous will drink from a cup [of wine] whose mixture is of Kāfūr,[1812]

[1812] A sweet-smelling spring in Paradise.
Suggest a correction

عَيْنًا يَشْرَبُ بِهَا عِبَادُ ٱللَّهِ يُفَجِّرُونَهَا تَفْجِيرًا ﴿٦﴾

6A spring of which the [righteous] servants of Allāh will drink; they will make it gush forth in force [and abundance].

Suggest a correction

يُوفُونَ بِٱلنَّذْرِ وَيَخَافُونَ يَوْمًا كَانَ شَرُّهُۥ مُسْتَطِيرًا ﴿٧﴾

7They [are those who] fulfill [their] vows and fear a Day whose evil will be widespread.

Suggest a correction

وَيُطْعِمُونَ ٱلطَّعَامَ عَلَىٰ حُبِّهِۦ مِسْكِينًا وَيَتِيمًا وَأَسِيرًا ﴿٨﴾

8And they give food in spite of love for it[1813] to the needy, the orphan, and the captive,

[1813] The meaning here may also be "out of love for Him," i.e., Allāh (subḥānahu wa taʿālā).
Suggest a correction

إِنَّمَا نُطْعِمُكُمْ لِوَجْهِ ٱللَّهِ لَا نُرِيدُ مِنكُمْ جَزَآءً وَلَا شُكُورًا ﴿٩﴾

9[Saying], "We feed you only for the face [i.e., approval] of Allāh. We wish not from you reward or gratitude.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّا نَخَافُ مِن رَّبِّنَا يَوْمًا عَبُوسًا قَمْطَرِيرًا ﴿١٠﴾

10Indeed, We fear from our Lord a Day austere and distressful."

Suggest a correction

فَوَقَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ شَرَّ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلْيَوْمِ وَلَقَّىٰهُمْ نَضْرَةً وَسُرُورًا ﴿١١﴾

11So Allāh will protect them from the evil of that Day and give them radiance and happiness

Suggest a correction

وَجَزَىٰهُم بِمَا صَبَرُوا۟ جَنَّةً وَحَرِيرًا ﴿١٢﴾

12And will reward them for what they patiently endured [with] a garden [in Paradise] and silk [garments].

Suggest a correction

مُّتَّكِـِٔينَ فِيهَا عَلَى ٱلْأَرَآئِكِ ۖ لَا يَرَوْنَ فِيهَا شَمْسًا وَلَا زَمْهَرِيرًا ﴿١٣﴾

13[They will be] reclining therein on adorned couches. They will not see therein any [burning] sun or [freezing] cold.

Suggest a correction

وَدَانِيَةً عَلَيْهِمْ ظِلَـٰلُهَا وَذُلِّلَتْ قُطُوفُهَا تَذْلِيلًا ﴿١٤﴾

14And near above them are its shades, and its [fruit] to be picked will be lowered in compliance.

Suggest a correction

وَيُطَافُ عَلَيْهِم بِـَٔانِيَةٍ مِّن فِضَّةٍ وَأَكْوَابٍ كَانَتْ قَوَارِيرَا۠ ﴿١٥﴾

15And there will be circulated among them vessels of silver and cups having been [created] clear [as glass],

Suggest a correction

قَوَارِيرَا۟ مِن فِضَّةٍ قَدَّرُوهَا تَقْدِيرًا ﴿١٦﴾

16Clear glasses [made] from silver of which they have determined the measure.

Suggest a correction

وَيُسْقَوْنَ فِيهَا كَأْسًا كَانَ مِزَاجُهَا زَنجَبِيلًا ﴿١٧﴾

17And they will be given to drink a cup [of wine] whose mixture is of ginger

Suggest a correction

عَيْنًا فِيهَا تُسَمَّىٰ سَلْسَبِيلًا ﴿١٨﴾

18[From] a fountain within it [i.e., Paradise] named Salsabeel.

Suggest a correction

وَيَطُوفُ عَلَيْهِمْ وِلْدَٰنٌ مُّخَلَّدُونَ إِذَا رَأَيْتَهُمْ حَسِبْتَهُمْ لُؤْلُؤًا مَّنثُورًا ﴿١٩﴾

19There will circulate among them young boys made eternal. When you see them, you would think them [as beautiful as] scattered pearls.

Suggest a correction

وَإِذَا رَأَيْتَ ثَمَّ رَأَيْتَ نَعِيمًا وَمُلْكًا كَبِيرًا ﴿٢٠﴾

20And when you look there [in Paradise], you will see pleasure and great dominion.

Suggest a correction

عَـٰلِيَهُمْ ثِيَابُ سُندُسٍ خُضْرٌ وَإِسْتَبْرَقٌ ۖ وَحُلُّوٓا۟ أَسَاوِرَ مِن فِضَّةٍ وَسَقَىٰهُمْ رَبُّهُمْ شَرَابًا طَهُورًا ﴿٢١﴾

21Upon them [i.e., the inhabitants] will be green garments of fine silk and brocade. And they will be adorned with bracelets of silver, and their Lord will give them a purifying drink.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّ هَـٰذَا كَانَ لَكُمْ جَزَآءً وَكَانَ سَعْيُكُم مَّشْكُورًا ﴿٢٢﴾

22[And it will be said], "Indeed, this is for you a reward, and your effort has been appreciated."

Suggest a correction

إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا عَلَيْكَ ٱلْقُرْءَانَ تَنزِيلًا ﴿٢٣﴾

23Indeed, it is We who have sent down to you, [O Muḥammad], the Qur’ān progressively.

Suggest a correction

فَٱصْبِرْ لِحُكْمِ رَبِّكَ وَلَا تُطِعْ مِنْهُمْ ءَاثِمًا أَوْ كَفُورًا ﴿٢٤﴾

24So be patient for the decision of your Lord and do not obey from among them a sinner or ungrateful [disbeliever].

Suggest a correction

وَٱذْكُرِ ٱسْمَ رَبِّكَ بُكْرَةً وَأَصِيلًا ﴿٢٥﴾

25And mention the name of your Lord [in prayer] morning and evening

Suggest a correction

وَمِنَ ٱلَّيْلِ فَٱسْجُدْ لَهُۥ وَسَبِّحْهُ لَيْلًا طَوِيلًا ﴿٢٦﴾

26And during the night prostrate to Him and exalt [i.e., praise] Him a long [part of the] night.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّ هَـٰٓؤُلَآءِ يُحِبُّونَ ٱلْعَاجِلَةَ وَيَذَرُونَ وَرَآءَهُمْ يَوْمًا ثَقِيلًا ﴿٢٧﴾

27Indeed, these [disbelievers] love the immediate and leave behind them[1814] a grave Day.

[1814] i.e., neglect. The meaning may also be "leave ahead of them."
Suggest a correction

نَّحْنُ خَلَقْنَـٰهُمْ وَشَدَدْنَآ أَسْرَهُمْ ۖ وَإِذَا شِئْنَا بَدَّلْنَآ أَمْثَـٰلَهُمْ تَبْدِيلًا ﴿٢٨﴾

28We have created them and strengthened their forms, and when We will, We can change their likenesses with [complete] alteration.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّ هَـٰذِهِۦ تَذْكِرَةٌ ۖ فَمَن شَآءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ سَبِيلًا ﴿٢٩﴾

29Indeed, this is a reminder, so he who wills may take to his Lord a way.

Suggest a correction

وَمَا تَشَآءُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمًا ﴿٣٠﴾

30And you do not will except that Allāh wills. Indeed, Allāh is ever Knowing and Wise.

Suggest a correction

يُدْخِلُ مَن يَشَآءُ فِى رَحْمَتِهِۦ ۚ وَٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ أَعَدَّ لَهُمْ عَذَابًا أَلِيمًۢا ﴿٣١﴾

31He admits whom He wills into His mercy; but the wrongdoers - He has prepared for them a painful punishment.
