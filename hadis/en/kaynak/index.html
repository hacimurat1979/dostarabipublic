HadeethEnc's terms

Under the terms on the HadeethEnc site, content may be republished unchanged; the publisher and source are credited; the version is stated and kept in the document; the source is notified; the content is updated to the latest version; there are no inappropriate ads. This site shows the content unchanged and states the source, link and retrieval date for every hadith; if the selection is retrieved in a new version, the pages are regenerated.

Version and retrieval

Source: API v1. There is no per-hadith version field; the retrieval date (2026-11-17) is recorded instead of a version.

Languages

Turkish and English exist for all 768 hadiths, Portuguese for 516; for the 252 hadiths without Portuguese the page shows the Turkish text and says so (no translation is invented). The source does not state whether HadeethEnc's Portuguese is Brazilian Portuguese; this is unverified.

Scope of the selection

The selection is HadeethEnc's 768 selected authentic hadiths; it is not a complete hadith collection. The Six Books (Kutub al-Sitta) are not covered in this section. Topic pages are filtered from this selection; having no hadith on a topic does not mean the topic is absent from the hadiths.

Grading and isnad

The grading shown with each hadith is the single value in the HadeethEnc record and is HadeethEnc's OWN assessment; it is not a comparison among scholars. This site does not grade, interpret the grading or reduce it to one ruling. “Narration/attribution” (for example “Narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim”) is not a grading and is shown with a separate label. This data has no isnad or narrator information, and this site has none.

Not a fatwa or ruling

These pages give no religious ruling and do not interpret hadiths; they show the source's text and record.
