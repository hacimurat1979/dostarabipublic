وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَهُوَ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ ﴿١٥٩﴾

159And indeed, your Lord - He is the Exalted in Might, the Merciful.

Suggest a correction

كَذَّبَتْ قَوْمُ لُوطٍ ٱلْمُرْسَلِينَ ﴿١٦٠﴾

160The people of Lot denied the messengers

Suggest a correction

إِذْ قَالَ لَهُمْ أَخُوهُمْ لُوطٌ أَلَا تَتَّقُونَ ﴿١٦١﴾

161When their brother Lot said to them, "Will you not fear Allāh?

Suggest a correction

إِنِّى لَكُمْ رَسُولٌ أَمِينٌ ﴿١٦٢﴾

162Indeed, I am to you a trustworthy messenger.

Suggest a correction

فَٱتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُونِ ﴿١٦٣﴾

163So fear Allāh and obey me.

Suggest a correction

وَمَآ أَسْـَٔلُكُمْ عَلَيْهِ مِنْ أَجْرٍ ۖ إِنْ أَجْرِىَ إِلَّا عَلَىٰ رَبِّ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ ﴿١٦٤﴾

164And I do not ask you for it any payment. My payment is only from the Lord of the worlds.

Suggest a correction

أَتَأْتُونَ ٱلذُّكْرَانَ مِنَ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ ﴿١٦٥﴾

165Do you approach males among the worlds[1057]

[1057] i.e., Are there, out of all Allāh's creatures, any besides you who commit this unnatural act?
Suggest a correction

وَتَذَرُونَ مَا خَلَقَ لَكُمْ رَبُّكُم مِّنْ أَزْوَٰجِكُم ۚ بَلْ أَنتُمْ قَوْمٌ عَادُونَ ﴿١٦٦﴾

166And leave what your Lord has created for you as mates? But you are a people transgressing."

Suggest a correction

قَالُوا۟ لَئِن لَّمْ تَنتَهِ يَـٰلُوطُ لَتَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلْمُخْرَجِينَ ﴿١٦٧﴾

167They said, "If you do not desist, O Lot, you will surely be of those evicted."

Suggest a correction

قَالَ إِنِّى لِعَمَلِكُم مِّنَ ٱلْقَالِينَ ﴿١٦٨﴾

168He said, "Indeed, I am, toward your deed, of those who detest [it].

Suggest a correction

رَبِّ نَجِّنِى وَأَهْلِى مِمَّا يَعْمَلُونَ ﴿١٦٩﴾

169My Lord, save me and my family from [the consequence of] what they do."

Suggest a correction

فَنَجَّيْنَـٰهُ وَأَهْلَهُۥٓ أَجْمَعِينَ ﴿١٧٠﴾

170So We saved him and his family, all,

Suggest a correction

إِلَّا عَجُوزًا فِى ٱلْغَـٰبِرِينَ ﴿١٧١﴾

171Except an old woman[1058] among those who remained behind.

[1058] Lot's wife, who had collaborated with the evildoers.
Suggest a correction

ثُمَّ دَمَّرْنَا ٱلْـَٔاخَرِينَ ﴿١٧٢﴾

172Then We destroyed the others.

Suggest a correction

وَأَمْطَرْنَا عَلَيْهِم مَّطَرًا ۖ فَسَآءَ مَطَرُ ٱلْمُنذَرِينَ ﴿١٧٣﴾

173And We rained upon them a rain [of stones], and evil was the rain of those who were warned.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّ فِى ذَٰلِكَ لَـَٔايَةً ۖ وَمَا كَانَ أَكْثَرُهُم مُّؤْمِنِينَ ﴿١٧٤﴾

174Indeed in that is a sign, but most of them were not to be believers.

Suggest a correction

وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَهُوَ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ ﴿١٧٥﴾

175And indeed, your Lord - He is the Exalted in Might, the Merciful.

Suggest a correction

كَذَّبَ أَصْحَـٰبُ لْـَٔيْكَةِ ٱلْمُرْسَلِينَ ﴿١٧٦﴾

176The companions of the thicket [i.e., the people of Madyan] denied the messengers

Suggest a correction

إِذْ قَالَ لَهُمْ شُعَيْبٌ أَلَا تَتَّقُونَ ﴿١٧٧﴾

177When Shuʿayb said to them, "Will you not fear Allāh?

Suggest a correction

إِنِّى لَكُمْ رَسُولٌ أَمِينٌ ﴿١٧٨﴾

178Indeed, I am to you a trustworthy messenger.

Suggest a correction

فَٱتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُونِ ﴿١٧٩﴾

179So fear Allāh and obey me.

Suggest a correction

وَمَآ أَسْـَٔلُكُمْ عَلَيْهِ مِنْ أَجْرٍ ۖ إِنْ أَجْرِىَ إِلَّا عَلَىٰ رَبِّ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ ﴿١٨٠﴾

180And I do not ask you for it any payment. My payment is only from the Lord of the worlds.

Suggest a correction

أَوْفُوا۟ ٱلْكَيْلَ وَلَا تَكُونُوا۟ مِنَ ٱلْمُخْسِرِينَ ﴿١٨١﴾

181Give full measure and do not be of those who cause loss.

Suggest a correction

وَزِنُوا۟ بِٱلْقِسْطَاسِ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمِ ﴿١٨٢﴾

182And weigh with an even [i.e., honest] balance.

Suggest a correction

وَلَا تَبْخَسُوا۟ ٱلنَّاسَ أَشْيَآءَهُمْ وَلَا تَعْثَوْا۟ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ مُفْسِدِينَ ﴿١٨٣﴾

183And do not deprive people of their due and do not commit abuse on earth, spreading corruption.

Suggest a correction

وَٱتَّقُوا۟ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَكُمْ وَٱلْجِبِلَّةَ ٱلْأَوَّلِينَ ﴿١٨٤﴾

184And fear He who created you and the former creation."[1059]

[1059] i.e., previous generations.
Suggest a correction

قَالُوٓا۟ إِنَّمَآ أَنتَ مِنَ ٱلْمُسَحَّرِينَ ﴿١٨٥﴾

185They said, "You are only of those affected by magic.

Suggest a correction

وَمَآ أَنتَ إِلَّا بَشَرٌ مِّثْلُنَا وَإِن نَّظُنُّكَ لَمِنَ ٱلْكَـٰذِبِينَ ﴿١٨٦﴾

186You are but a man like ourselves, and indeed, we think you are among the liars.

Suggest a correction

فَأَسْقِطْ عَلَيْنَا كِسَفًا مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ إِن كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلصَّـٰدِقِينَ ﴿١٨٧﴾

187So cause to fall upon us fragments of the sky, if you should be of the truthful."

Suggest a correction

قَالَ رَبِّىٓ أَعْلَمُ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ ﴿١٨٨﴾

188He said, "My Lord is most knowing of what you do."

Suggest a correction

فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَأَخَذَهُمْ عَذَابُ يَوْمِ ٱلظُّلَّةِ ۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ عَذَابَ يَوْمٍ عَظِيمٍ ﴿١٨٩﴾

189And they denied him, so the punishment of the day of the black cloud seized them. Indeed, it was the punishment of a terrible day.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّ فِى ذَٰلِكَ لَـَٔايَةً ۖ وَمَا كَانَ أَكْثَرُهُم مُّؤْمِنِينَ ﴿١٩٠﴾

190Indeed in that is a sign, but most of them were not to be believers.

Suggest a correction

وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَهُوَ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ ﴿١٩١﴾

191And indeed, your Lord - He is the Exalted in Might, the Merciful.

Suggest a correction

وَإِنَّهُۥ لَتَنزِيلُ رَبِّ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ ﴿١٩٢﴾

192And indeed, it [i.e., the Qur’ān] is the revelation of the Lord of the worlds.

Suggest a correction

نَزَلَ بِهِ ٱلرُّوحُ ٱلْأَمِينُ ﴿١٩٣﴾

193The Trustworthy Spirit [i.e., Gabriel] has brought it down

Suggest a correction

عَلَىٰ قَلْبِكَ لِتَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلْمُنذِرِينَ ﴿١٩٤﴾

194Upon your heart, [O Muḥammad] - that you may be of the warners

Suggest a correction

بِلِسَانٍ عَرَبِىٍّ مُّبِينٍ ﴿١٩٥﴾

195In a clear Arabic language.

Suggest a correction

وَإِنَّهُۥ لَفِى زُبُرِ ٱلْأَوَّلِينَ ﴿١٩٦﴾

196And indeed, it is [mentioned] in the scriptures of former peoples.

Suggest a correction

أَوَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُمْ ءَايَةً أَن يَعْلَمَهُۥ عُلَمَـٰٓؤُا۟ بَنِىٓ إِسْرَٰٓءِيلَ ﴿١٩٧﴾

197And has it not been a sign to them that it is recognized by the scholars of the Children of Israel?

Suggest a correction

وَلَوْ نَزَّلْنَـٰهُ عَلَىٰ بَعْضِ ٱلْأَعْجَمِينَ ﴿١٩٨﴾

198And even if We had revealed it to one among the foreigners[1060]

[1060] i.e., the non-Arabs or those who are not fluent in the Arabic language.
Suggest a correction

فَقَرَأَهُۥ عَلَيْهِم مَّا كَانُوا۟ بِهِۦ مُؤْمِنِينَ ﴿١٩٩﴾

199And he had recited it to them [perfectly],[1061] they would [still] not have been believers in it.

[1061] As a miracle from Allāh.
Suggest a correction

كَذَٰلِكَ سَلَكْنَـٰهُ فِى قُلُوبِ ٱلْمُجْرِمِينَ ﴿٢٠٠﴾

200Thus have We inserted it [i.e., disbelief] into the hearts of the criminals.

Suggest a correction

لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ بِهِۦ حَتَّىٰ يَرَوُا۟ ٱلْعَذَابَ ٱلْأَلِيمَ ﴿٢٠١﴾

201They will not believe in it until they see the painful punishment.

Suggest a correction

فَيَأْتِيَهُم بَغْتَةً وَهُمْ لَا يَشْعُرُونَ ﴿٢٠٢﴾

202And it will come to them suddenly while they perceive [it] not.

Suggest a correction

فَيَقُولُوا۟ هَلْ نَحْنُ مُنظَرُونَ ﴿٢٠٣﴾

203And they will say, "May we be reprieved?"

Suggest a correction

أَفَبِعَذَابِنَا يَسْتَعْجِلُونَ ﴿٢٠٤﴾

204So for Our punishment are they impatient?

Suggest a correction

أَفَرَءَيْتَ إِن مَّتَّعْنَـٰهُمْ سِنِينَ ﴿٢٠٥﴾

205Then have you considered if We gave them enjoyment for years

Suggest a correction

ثُمَّ جَآءَهُم مَّا كَانُوا۟ يُوعَدُونَ ﴿٢٠٦﴾

206And then there came to them that which they were promised?

Suggest a correction

مَآ أَغْنَىٰ عَنْهُم مَّا كَانُوا۟ يُمَتَّعُونَ ﴿٢٠٧﴾

207They would not be availed by the enjoyment with which they were provided.

Suggest a correction

وَمَآ أَهْلَكْنَا مِن قَرْيَةٍ إِلَّا لَهَا مُنذِرُونَ ﴿٢٠٨﴾

208And We did not destroy any city except that it had warners

Suggest a correction

ذِكْرَىٰ وَمَا كُنَّا ظَـٰلِمِينَ ﴿٢٠٩﴾

209As a reminder; and never have We been unjust.

Suggest a correction

وَمَا تَنَزَّلَتْ بِهِ ٱلشَّيَـٰطِينُ ﴿٢١٠﴾

210And the devils have not brought it [i.e., the revelation] down.[1062]

[1062] As was asserted by the disbelievers. Rather, it was brought by Gabriel, the Trustworthy Spirit.
Suggest a correction

وَمَا يَنۢبَغِى لَهُمْ وَمَا يَسْتَطِيعُونَ ﴿٢١١﴾

211It is not allowable for them, nor would they be able.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّهُمْ عَنِ ٱلسَّمْعِ لَمَعْزُولُونَ ﴿٢١٢﴾

212Indeed they, from [its] hearing, are removed.[1063]

[1063] As mentioned in 72:9.
Suggest a correction

فَلَا تَدْعُ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَـٰهًا ءَاخَرَ فَتَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلْمُعَذَّبِينَ ﴿٢١٣﴾

213So do not invoke[1064] with Allāh another deity and [thus] be among the punished.

[1064] Or "worship."
Suggest a correction

وَأَنذِرْ عَشِيرَتَكَ ٱلْأَقْرَبِينَ ﴿٢١٤﴾

214And warn, [O Muḥammad], your closest kindred.

Suggest a correction

وَٱخْفِضْ جَنَاحَكَ لِمَنِ ٱتَّبَعَكَ مِنَ ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ ﴿٢١٥﴾

215And lower your wing [i.e., show kindness] to those who follow you of the believers.

Suggest a correction

فَإِنْ عَصَوْكَ فَقُلْ إِنِّى بَرِىٓءٌ مِّمَّا تَعْمَلُونَ ﴿٢١٦﴾

216And if they disobey you, then say, "Indeed, I am disassociated from what you are doing."

Suggest a correction

وَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى ٱلْعَزِيزِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ ﴿٢١٧﴾

217And rely upon the Exalted in Might, the Merciful,

Suggest a correction

ٱلَّذِى يَرَىٰكَ حِينَ تَقُومُ ﴿٢١٨﴾

218Who sees you when you arise[1065]

[1065] From your bed at night for prayer while you are alone.
Suggest a correction

وَتَقَلُّبَكَ فِى ٱلسَّـٰجِدِينَ ﴿٢١٩﴾

219And your movement among those who prostrate.[1066]

[1066] i.e., among those who pray with you in congregation.
Suggest a correction

إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلْعَلِيمُ ﴿٢٢٠﴾

220Indeed, He is the Hearing, the Knowing.

Suggest a correction

هَلْ أُنَبِّئُكُمْ عَلَىٰ مَن تَنَزَّلُ ٱلشَّيَـٰطِينُ ﴿٢٢١﴾

221Shall I inform you upon whom the devils descend?

Suggest a correction

تَنَزَّلُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ أَفَّاكٍ أَثِيمٍ ﴿٢٢٢﴾

222They descend upon every sinful liar.

Suggest a correction

يُلْقُونَ ٱلسَّمْعَ وَأَكْثَرُهُمْ كَـٰذِبُونَ ﴿٢٢٣﴾

223They pass on what is heard,[1067] and most of them are liars.

[1067] This was before they were prevented, as described in 72:8-9.
Suggest a correction

وَٱلشُّعَرَآءُ يَتَّبِعُهُمُ ٱلْغَاوُۥنَ ﴿٢٢٤﴾

224And the poets - [only] the deviators follow them.

Suggest a correction

أَلَمْ تَرَ أَنَّهُمْ فِى كُلِّ وَادٍ يَهِيمُونَ ﴿٢٢٥﴾

225Do you not see that in every valley they roam[1068]

[1068] Speaking lies indiscriminately and praising and disparaging others according to whim.
Suggest a correction

وَأَنَّهُمْ يَقُولُونَ مَا لَا يَفْعَلُونَ ﴿٢٢٦﴾

226And that they say what they do not do? -

Suggest a correction

إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَـٰتِ وَذَكَرُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرًا وَٱنتَصَرُوا۟ مِنۢ بَعْدِ مَا ظُلِمُوا۟ ۗ وَسَيَعْلَمُ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُوٓا۟ أَىَّ مُنقَلَبٍ يَنقَلِبُونَ ﴿٢٢٧﴾

227Except those [poets] who believe and do righteous deeds and remember Allāh often and defend [the Muslims][1069] after they were wronged. And those who have wronged are going to know to what [kind of] return they will be returned.

[1069] By replying through poetry to the attacks of hostile poets.
