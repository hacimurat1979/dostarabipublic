بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

أَتَىٰٓ أَمْرُ ٱللَّهِ فَلَا تَسْتَعْجِلُوهُ ۚ سُبْحَـٰنَهُۥ وَتَعَـٰلَىٰ عَمَّا يُشْرِكُونَ ﴿١﴾

1The command of Allāh is coming,[690] so be not impatient for it. Exalted is He and high above what they associate with Him.

[690] Literally, "has come," indicating the certainty and nearness of the Last Hour.
