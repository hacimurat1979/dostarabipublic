Parallel reading table

This section is not a reading summary. It is an attempt to read Dost's words side by side with the findings of physics that do not fit reason; it is our own reading frame. We do not say “the same” or “confirmed”: we only ask, and we say where they part ways. The text is our own rendering of a Turkish original.

Two sides in two separate windows: on the left a summary of a physics paper, on the right a passage from Dost or a form page. You choose the pair; the site proposes no matching. Below you write your note and save the pair on your device. If you wish you put the pair and your note among the things to be sent to Claude; if you press Try to refute, Claude tries to refute the resemblance (does it appear in other texts too, on what grounds might it be wrong) and brings you the result. None of it is written to the site.
