Physics papers

This section is not a reading summary. It is an attempt to read Dost's words side by side with the findings of physics that do not fit reason; it is our own reading frame. We do not say “the same” or “confirmed”: we only ask, and we say where they part ways. The text is our own rendering of a Turkish original.

Sixty-three topics from the basic papers of quantum physics: the main question of each topic and plain-language summaries of its key papers. The aim is to give what these papers say in our own words, staying close to the source and adding no commentary; reading them side by side with other texts is left to the reader.

Each summary states the paper's question, what it did and its result; experiment, theory and interpretation are kept apart. Summaries written without looking at the paper's own abstract or text are marked. Every paper's citation is given with a source link; Sources page as well.
