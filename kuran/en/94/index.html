94 The Relief

Meccan · 8 verses · juz 30 · 1 ruku · revelation order 12

The Arabic text comes from Tanzil and the translations from QuranEnc, unchanged. A translation is not the Qur'an itself; the Arabic text is a separate block. This page is not a commentary or interpretation. Translation shown: Uluslararası Nur Merkezi

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

أَلَمْ نَشْرَحْ لَكَ صَدْرَكَ ﴿١﴾

1Did We not expand for you, [O Muḥammad], your breast?[1944]

[1944] i.e., enlighten, assure and gladden your heart with guidance.
Suggest a correction

وَوَضَعْنَا عَنكَ وِزْرَكَ ﴿٢﴾

2And We removed from you your burden[1945]

[1945] By forgiving any errors which you may have committed previously or might commit consequently. "Burden" can also refer to the anxiety experienced by the Prophet (ﷺ) at the beginning of his mission.
Suggest a correction

ٱلَّذِىٓ أَنقَضَ ظَهْرَكَ ﴿٣﴾

3Which had weighed upon your back

وَرَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَ ﴿٤﴾

4And raised high for you your repute.

فَإِنَّ مَعَ ٱلْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا ﴿٥﴾

5For indeed, with hardship [will be] ease [i.e., relief].

7So when you have finished [your duties], then stand up [for worship].
