Barzakh and Beyond

Where Dost speaks of the barzakh, the Reality-Earth, the Resurrection and what lies beyond

This page is a collection, not a reading summary. It gathers the places where Dost speaks of these subjects, by where they appear in the site's Futuhat and Fusus summaries. Words in quotation marks are Dost's (quotations from the summaries); everything else is our measurement and arrangement. Dividing the scenes and word groups this way is only for ease of reading; it is not an ordering or classification by Dost. The page draws no meaning.

Where the words appear

Each dot is a part or a chapter; its size shows how many times the word appears in that text. Press a dot to open that part or chapter. The strip shows what recurs, not what it means. The counts are an APPROXIMATE scan: Turkish words are searched in the summary texts; a word may also occur in another sense, and some inflected forms may not be counted. This is our measurement, not Dost's.

Limits

  • The counts are an approximate scan (see above); they are not exact figures.
  • The quotations come from the site's part and chapter summaries; they have not been checked against the Arabic original on this page.
  • A word that looks rare in the strip is not therefore unimportant: the count only measures where the word occurs. For example, the Reality-Earth is the name of a section of Volume I, Part XI, yet the word occurs in the summary texts in only a few places.
  • No order is claimed: scenes and words are grouped only for ease of reading.
