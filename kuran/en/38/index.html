38 The Letter "Saad"

Meccan · 88 verses · juz 23 · 5 ruku · revelation order 38

The Arabic text comes from Tanzil and the translations from QuranEnc, unchanged. A translation is not the Qur'an itself; the Arabic text is a separate block. This page is not a commentary or interpretation. Translation shown: Uluslararası Nur Merkezi

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

صٓ ۚ وَٱلْقُرْءَانِ ذِى ٱلذِّكْرِ ﴿١﴾

1Ṣād.[1316] By the Qur’ān containing reminder...[1317]

[1316] See footnote to 2:1. [1317] The completion of the oath is understood to be that the Qur’ān is inimitable and thus a miracle from Allāh.
