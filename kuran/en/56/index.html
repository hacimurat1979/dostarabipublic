56 The Inevitable

Meccan · 96 verses · juz 27 · 3 ruku · revelation order 46

The Arabic text comes from Tanzil and the translations from QuranEnc, unchanged. A translation is not the Qur'an itself; the Arabic text is a separate block. This page is not a commentary or interpretation. Translation shown: Uluslararası Nur Merkezi

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

إِذَا وَقَعَتِ ٱلْوَاقِعَةُ ﴿١﴾

1When the Occurrence occurs,

Suggest a correction

لَيْسَ لِوَقْعَتِهَا كَاذِبَةٌ ﴿٢﴾

2There is, at its occurrence, no denial.

Suggest a correction

خَافِضَةٌ رَّافِعَةٌ ﴿٣﴾

3It will bring down [some] and raise up [others].[1608]

[1608] According to their deeds rather than wealth and social position, as is the case in this world.
Suggest a correction

إِذَا رُجَّتِ ٱلْأَرْضُ رَجًّا ﴿٤﴾

4When the earth is shaken with convulsion

Suggest a correction

وَبُسَّتِ ٱلْجِبَالُ بَسًّا ﴿٥﴾

5And the mountains are broken down, crumbling

Suggest a correction

فَكَانَتْ هَبَآءً مُّنۢبَثًّا ﴿٦﴾

6And become dust dispersing,

Suggest a correction

وَكُنتُمْ أَزْوَٰجًا ثَلَـٰثَةً ﴿٧﴾

7And you become [of] three kinds:

Suggest a correction

فَأَصْحَـٰبُ ٱلْمَيْمَنَةِ مَآ أَصْحَـٰبُ ٱلْمَيْمَنَةِ ﴿٨﴾

8Then the companions of the right - what are the companions of the right?[1609]

[1609] i.e., those given their records in their right hand and who are destined for Paradise.
Suggest a correction

وَأَصْحَـٰبُ ٱلْمَشْـَٔمَةِ مَآ أَصْحَـٰبُ ٱلْمَشْـَٔمَةِ ﴿٩﴾

9And the companions of the left - what are companions of the left?[1610]

[1610] i.e., those given their records in their left hand and who are destined for Hell.
Suggest a correction

وَٱلسَّـٰبِقُونَ ٱلسَّـٰبِقُونَ ﴿١٠﴾

10And the forerunners, the forerunners[1611] -

[1611] The words can also be understood as a complete sentence, i.e., "The forerunners [in good deeds] are the forerunners [in entering Paradise]."
Suggest a correction

أُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلْمُقَرَّبُونَ ﴿١١﴾

11Those are the ones brought near [to Allāh]

Suggest a correction

فِى جَنَّـٰتِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ ﴿١٢﴾

12In the Gardens of Pleasure,

Suggest a correction

ثُلَّةٌ مِّنَ ٱلْأَوَّلِينَ ﴿١٣﴾

13A [large] company of the former peoples

Suggest a correction

وَقَلِيلٌ مِّنَ ٱلْـَٔاخِرِينَ ﴿١٤﴾

14And a few of the later peoples,

Suggest a correction

عَلَىٰ سُرُرٍ مَّوْضُونَةٍ ﴿١٥﴾

15On thrones woven [with ornament],

Suggest a correction

مُّتَّكِـِٔينَ عَلَيْهَا مُتَقَـٰبِلِينَ ﴿١٦﴾

16Reclining on them, facing each other.

Suggest a correction

يَطُوفُ عَلَيْهِمْ وِلْدَٰنٌ مُّخَلَّدُونَ ﴿١٧﴾

17There will circulate among them young boys made eternal

Suggest a correction

بِأَكْوَابٍ وَأَبَارِيقَ وَكَأْسٍ مِّن مَّعِينٍ ﴿١٨﴾

18With vessels, pitchers and a cup [of wine] from a flowing spring -

Suggest a correction

لَّا يُصَدَّعُونَ عَنْهَا وَلَا يُنزِفُونَ ﴿١٩﴾

19No headache will they have therefrom, nor will they be intoxicated -

Suggest a correction

وَفَـٰكِهَةٍ مِّمَّا يَتَخَيَّرُونَ ﴿٢٠﴾

20And fruit of what they select

Suggest a correction

وَلَحْمِ طَيْرٍ مِّمَّا يَشْتَهُونَ ﴿٢١﴾

21And the meat of fowl, from whatever they desire.

Suggest a correction

وَحُورٌ عِينٌ ﴿٢٢﴾

22And [for them are] fair women with large, [beautiful] eyes,

Suggest a correction

كَأَمْثَـٰلِ ٱللُّؤْلُؤِ ٱلْمَكْنُونِ ﴿٢٣﴾

23The likenesses of pearls well-protected,

Suggest a correction

جَزَآءًۢ بِمَا كَانُوا۟ يَعْمَلُونَ ﴿٢٤﴾

24As reward for what they used to do.

Suggest a correction

لَا يَسْمَعُونَ فِيهَا لَغْوًا وَلَا تَأْثِيمًا ﴿٢٥﴾

25They will not hear therein ill speech or commission of sin -

Suggest a correction

إِلَّا قِيلًا سَلَـٰمًا سَلَـٰمًا ﴿٢٦﴾

26Only a saying [of] peace, peace.

Suggest a correction

وَأَصْحَـٰبُ ٱلْيَمِينِ مَآ أَصْحَـٰبُ ٱلْيَمِينِ ﴿٢٧﴾

27The companions of the right - what are the companions of the right?

Suggest a correction

فِى سِدْرٍ مَّخْضُودٍ ﴿٢٨﴾

28[They will be] among lote trees with thorns removed

Suggest a correction

وَطَلْحٍ مَّنضُودٍ ﴿٢٩﴾

29And [banana] trees layered [with fruit]

Suggest a correction

وَظِلٍّ مَّمْدُودٍ ﴿٣٠﴾

30And shade extended

Suggest a correction

وَمَآءٍ مَّسْكُوبٍ ﴿٣١﴾

31And water poured out

Suggest a correction

وَفَـٰكِهَةٍ كَثِيرَةٍ ﴿٣٢﴾

32And fruit, abundant [and varied],

Suggest a correction

لَّا مَقْطُوعَةٍ وَلَا مَمْنُوعَةٍ ﴿٣٣﴾

33Neither limited [to season] nor forbidden,

Suggest a correction

وَفُرُشٍ مَّرْفُوعَةٍ ﴿٣٤﴾

34And [upon] beds raised high.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّآ أَنشَأْنَـٰهُنَّ إِنشَآءً ﴿٣٥﴾

35Indeed, We have produced them [i.e., the women of Paradise] in a [new] creation

Suggest a correction

فَجَعَلْنَـٰهُنَّ أَبْكَارًا ﴿٣٦﴾

36And made them virgins,

Suggest a correction

عُرُبًا أَتْرَابًا ﴿٣٧﴾

37Devoted [to their husbands] and of equal age,

Suggest a correction

لِّأَصْحَـٰبِ ٱلْيَمِينِ ﴿٣٨﴾

38For the companions of the right [who are]

Suggest a correction

ثُلَّةٌ مِّنَ ٱلْأَوَّلِينَ ﴿٣٩﴾

39A company of the former peoples

Suggest a correction

وَثُلَّةٌ مِّنَ ٱلْـَٔاخِرِينَ ﴿٤٠﴾

40And a company of the later peoples.

Suggest a correction

وَأَصْحَـٰبُ ٱلشِّمَالِ مَآ أَصْحَـٰبُ ٱلشِّمَالِ ﴿٤١﴾

41And the companions of the left - what are the companions of the left?

Suggest a correction

فِى سَمُومٍ وَحَمِيمٍ ﴿٤٢﴾

42[They will be] in scorching fire and scalding water

Suggest a correction

وَظِلٍّ مِّن يَحْمُومٍ ﴿٤٣﴾

43And a shade of black smoke,

Suggest a correction

لَّا بَارِدٍ وَلَا كَرِيمٍ ﴿٤٤﴾

44Neither cool nor beneficial.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّهُمْ كَانُوا۟ قَبْلَ ذَٰلِكَ مُتْرَفِينَ ﴿٤٥﴾

45Indeed they were, before that, indulging in affluence,

Suggest a correction

وَكَانُوا۟ يُصِرُّونَ عَلَى ٱلْحِنثِ ٱلْعَظِيمِ ﴿٤٦﴾

46And they used to persist in the great violation,[1612]

[1612] i.e., shirk (association with Allāh) or disbelief.
Suggest a correction

وَكَانُوا۟ يَقُولُونَ أَئِذَا مِتْنَا وَكُنَّا تُرَابًا وَعِظَـٰمًا أَءِنَّا لَمَبْعُوثُونَ ﴿٤٧﴾

47And they used to say, "When we die and become dust and bones, are we indeed to be resurrected?

Suggest a correction

أَوَءَابَآؤُنَا ٱلْأَوَّلُونَ ﴿٤٨﴾

48And our forefathers [as well]?"

Suggest a correction

قُلْ إِنَّ ٱلْأَوَّلِينَ وَٱلْـَٔاخِرِينَ ﴿٤٩﴾

49Say, [O Muḥammad], "Indeed, the former and later peoples

Suggest a correction

لَمَجْمُوعُونَ إِلَىٰ مِيقَـٰتِ يَوْمٍ مَّعْلُومٍ ﴿٥٠﴾

50Are to be gathered together for the appointment of a known Day."

Suggest a correction

ثُمَّ إِنَّكُمْ أَيُّهَا ٱلضَّآلُّونَ ٱلْمُكَذِّبُونَ ﴿٥١﴾

51Then indeed you, O those astray [who are] deniers,

Suggest a correction

لَـَٔاكِلُونَ مِن شَجَرٍ مِّن زَقُّومٍ ﴿٥٢﴾

52Will be eating from trees of zaqqūm

Suggest a correction

فَمَالِـُٔونَ مِنْهَا ٱلْبُطُونَ ﴿٥٣﴾

53And filling with it your bellies

Suggest a correction

فَشَـٰرِبُونَ عَلَيْهِ مِنَ ٱلْحَمِيمِ ﴿٥٤﴾

54And drinking on top of it from scalding water

Suggest a correction

فَشَـٰرِبُونَ شُرْبَ ٱلْهِيمِ ﴿٥٥﴾

55And will drink as the drinking of thirsty camels.

Suggest a correction

هَـٰذَا نُزُلُهُمْ يَوْمَ ٱلدِّينِ ﴿٥٦﴾

56That is their accommodation on the Day of Recompense.

Suggest a correction

نَحْنُ خَلَقْنَـٰكُمْ فَلَوْلَا تُصَدِّقُونَ ﴿٥٧﴾

57We have created you, so why do you not believe?

Suggest a correction

أَفَرَءَيْتُم مَّا تُمْنُونَ ﴿٥٨﴾

58Have you seen that which you emit?[1613]

[1613] i.e., semen, which contains the potential for human life.
Suggest a correction

ءَأَنتُمْ تَخْلُقُونَهُۥٓ أَمْ نَحْنُ ٱلْخَـٰلِقُونَ ﴿٥٩﴾

59Is it you who creates it, or are We the Creator?

Suggest a correction

نَحْنُ قَدَّرْنَا بَيْنَكُمُ ٱلْمَوْتَ وَمَا نَحْنُ بِمَسْبُوقِينَ ﴿٦٠﴾

60We have decreed death among you, and We are not to be outdone

Suggest a correction

عَلَىٰٓ أَن نُّبَدِّلَ أَمْثَـٰلَكُمْ وَنُنشِئَكُمْ فِى مَا لَا تَعْلَمُونَ ﴿٦١﴾

61In that We will change your likenesses and produce you in that [form] which you do not know.[1614]

[1614] An alternative meaning has also been given: "...in that We will replace the likes of you [with others upon the earth] and create you [in the Hereafter] in that which you do not know."
Suggest a correction

وَلَقَدْ عَلِمْتُمُ ٱلنَّشْأَةَ ٱلْأُولَىٰ فَلَوْلَا تَذَكَّرُونَ ﴿٦٢﴾

62And you have already known the first creation, so will you not remember?

Suggest a correction

أَفَرَءَيْتُم مَّا تَحْرُثُونَ ﴿٦٣﴾

63And have you seen that [seed] which you sow?

Suggest a correction

ءَأَنتُمْ تَزْرَعُونَهُۥٓ أَمْ نَحْنُ ٱلزَّٰرِعُونَ ﴿٦٤﴾

64Is it you who makes it grow, or are We the grower?

Suggest a correction

لَوْ نَشَآءُ لَجَعَلْنَـٰهُ حُطَـٰمًا فَظَلْتُمْ تَفَكَّهُونَ ﴿٦٥﴾

65If We willed, We could make it [dry] debris, and you would remain in wonder,[1615]

[1615] At what had happened or remain in a state of shock. Another meaning is "in regret."
Suggest a correction

إِنَّا لَمُغْرَمُونَ ﴿٦٦﴾

66[Saying], "Indeed, we are [now] in debt;

Suggest a correction

بَلْ نَحْنُ مَحْرُومُونَ ﴿٦٧﴾

67Rather, we have been deprived."

Suggest a correction

أَفَرَءَيْتُمُ ٱلْمَآءَ ٱلَّذِى تَشْرَبُونَ ﴿٦٨﴾

68And have you seen the water that you drink?

Suggest a correction

ءَأَنتُمْ أَنزَلْتُمُوهُ مِنَ ٱلْمُزْنِ أَمْ نَحْنُ ٱلْمُنزِلُونَ ﴿٦٩﴾

69Is it you who brought it down from the clouds, or is it We who bring it down?

Suggest a correction

لَوْ نَشَآءُ جَعَلْنَـٰهُ أُجَاجًا فَلَوْلَا تَشْكُرُونَ ﴿٧٠﴾

70If We willed, We could make it bitter, so why are you not grateful?

Suggest a correction

أَفَرَءَيْتُمُ ٱلنَّارَ ٱلَّتِى تُورُونَ ﴿٧١﴾

71And have you seen the fire that you ignite?

Suggest a correction

ءَأَنتُمْ أَنشَأْتُمْ شَجَرَتَهَآ أَمْ نَحْنُ ٱلْمُنشِـُٔونَ ﴿٧٢﴾

72Is it you who produced its tree, or are We the producer?

Suggest a correction

نَحْنُ جَعَلْنَـٰهَا تَذْكِرَةً وَمَتَـٰعًا لِّلْمُقْوِينَ ﴿٧٣﴾

73We have made it a reminder[1616] and provision for the travelers,[1617]

[1616] Of the great fire of Hell. [1617] In the form of flints or other means by which to ignite fire. Travelers are mentioned because of the special convenience to them, although it is a provision for all people in general.
Suggest a correction

فَسَبِّحْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلْعَظِيمِ ﴿٧٤﴾

74So exalt the name of your Lord, the Most Great.

Suggest a correction

فَلَآ أُقْسِمُ بِمَوَٰقِعِ ٱلنُّجُومِ ﴿٧٥﴾

75Then I swear by the setting of the stars,[1618]

[1618] Allāh (subḥānahu wa taʿālā) confirms absolutely by oath.
Suggest a correction

وَإِنَّهُۥ لَقَسَمٌ لَّوْ تَعْلَمُونَ عَظِيمٌ ﴿٧٦﴾

76And indeed, it is an oath - if you could know - [most] great.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّهُۥ لَقُرْءَانٌ كَرِيمٌ ﴿٧٧﴾

77Indeed, it is a noble Qur’ān.

Suggest a correction

فِى كِتَـٰبٍ مَّكْنُونٍ ﴿٧٨﴾

78In a Register well-protected;[1619].

[1619] The Preserved Slate (al-Lawḥ al-Maḥfūẓ), which is with Allāh (subḥānahu wa taʿālā).
Suggest a correction

لَّا يَمَسُّهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱلْمُطَهَّرُونَ ﴿٧٩﴾

79None touch it except the purified [i.e., the angels].

Suggest a correction

تَنزِيلٌ مِّن رَّبِّ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ ﴿٨٠﴾

80[It is] a revelation from the Lord of the worlds.

Suggest a correction

أَفَبِهَـٰذَا ٱلْحَدِيثِ أَنتُم مُّدْهِنُونَ ﴿٨١﴾

81Then is it to this statement that you are indifferent

Suggest a correction

وَتَجْعَلُونَ رِزْقَكُمْ أَنَّكُمْ تُكَذِّبُونَ ﴿٨٢﴾

82And make [the thanks for] your provision that you deny [the Provider]?

Suggest a correction

فَلَوْلَآ إِذَا بَلَغَتِ ٱلْحُلْقُومَ ﴿٨٣﴾

83Then why, when it [i.e., the soul at death] reaches the throat

Suggest a correction

وَأَنتُمْ حِينَئِذٍ تَنظُرُونَ ﴿٨٤﴾

84And you are at that time looking on -

Suggest a correction

وَنَحْنُ أَقْرَبُ إِلَيْهِ مِنكُمْ وَلَـٰكِن لَّا تُبْصِرُونَ ﴿٨٥﴾

85And We [i.e., Our angels] are nearer to him than you, but you do not see -

Suggest a correction

فَلَوْلَآ إِن كُنتُمْ غَيْرَ مَدِينِينَ ﴿٨٦﴾

86Then why do you not, if you are not to be recompensed,

Suggest a correction

تَرْجِعُونَهَآ إِن كُنتُمْ صَـٰدِقِينَ ﴿٨٧﴾

87Bring it back,[1620] if you should be truthful?

[1620] i.e., return the soul to the body, meaning that just as you cannot prevent death when it is decreed, you will not escape the recompense when it is decreed.
Suggest a correction

فَأَمَّآ إِن كَانَ مِنَ ٱلْمُقَرَّبِينَ ﴿٨٨﴾

88And if he [i.e., the deceased] was of those brought near [to Allāh],

Suggest a correction

فَرَوْحٌ وَرَيْحَانٌ وَجَنَّتُ نَعِيمٍ ﴿٨٩﴾

89Then [for him is] rest and bounty and a garden of pleasure.

Suggest a correction

وَأَمَّآ إِن كَانَ مِنْ أَصْحَـٰبِ ٱلْيَمِينِ ﴿٩٠﴾

90And if he was of the companions of the right,

Suggest a correction

فَسَلَـٰمٌ لَّكَ مِنْ أَصْحَـٰبِ ٱلْيَمِينِ ﴿٩١﴾

91Then [the angels will say], "Peace for you; [you are] from the companions of the right."

Suggest a correction

وَأَمَّآ إِن كَانَ مِنَ ٱلْمُكَذِّبِينَ ٱلضَّآلِّينَ ﴿٩٢﴾

92But if he was of the deniers [who were] astray,

Suggest a correction

فَنُزُلٌ مِّنْ حَمِيمٍ ﴿٩٣﴾

93Then [for him is] accommodation of scalding water

Suggest a correction

وَتَصْلِيَةُ جَحِيمٍ ﴿٩٤﴾

94And burning in Hellfire.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّ هَـٰذَا لَهُوَ حَقُّ ٱلْيَقِينِ ﴿٩٥﴾

95Indeed, this is the true certainty,

Suggest a correction

فَسَبِّحْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلْعَظِيمِ ﴿٩٦﴾

96So exalt the name of your Lord, the Most Great.

Suggest a correction
