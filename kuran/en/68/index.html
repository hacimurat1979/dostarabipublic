68 The Pen

Meccan · 52 verses · juz 29 · 2 ruku · revelation order 2

The Arabic text comes from Tanzil and the translations from QuranEnc, unchanged. A translation is not the Qur'an itself; the Arabic text is a separate block. This page is not a commentary or interpretation. Translation shown: Uluslararası Nur Merkezi

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

نٓ ۚ وَٱلْقَلَمِ وَمَا يَسْطُرُونَ ﴿١﴾

1Nūn.[1724] By the pen and what they inscribe,

[1724] See footnote to 2:1.
Suggest a correction

مَآ أَنتَ بِنِعْمَةِ رَبِّكَ بِمَجْنُونٍ ﴿٢﴾

2You are not, [O Muḥammad], by the favor of your Lord, a madman.

Suggest a correction

وَإِنَّ لَكَ لَأَجْرًا غَيْرَ مَمْنُونٍ ﴿٣﴾

3And indeed, for you is a reward uninterrupted.

Suggest a correction

وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلَىٰ خُلُقٍ عَظِيمٍ ﴿٤﴾

4And indeed, you are of a great moral character.

Suggest a correction

فَسَتُبْصِرُ وَيُبْصِرُونَ ﴿٥﴾

5So you will see and they will see

Suggest a correction

بِأَييِّكُمُ ٱلْمَفْتُونُ ﴿٦﴾

6Which of you is the afflicted [by a devil].

Suggest a correction

إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ أَعْلَمُ بِمَن ضَلَّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦ وَهُوَ أَعْلَمُ بِٱلْمُهْتَدِينَ ﴿٧﴾

7Indeed, your Lord is most knowing of who has gone astray from His way, and He is most knowing of the [rightly] guided.

Suggest a correction

فَلَا تُطِعِ ٱلْمُكَذِّبِينَ ﴿٨﴾

8Then do not obey the deniers.

Suggest a correction

وَدُّوا۟ لَوْ تُدْهِنُ فَيُدْهِنُونَ ﴿٩﴾

9They wish that you would soften [in your position], so they would soften [toward you].

Suggest a correction

وَلَا تُطِعْ كُلَّ حَلَّافٍ مَّهِينٍ ﴿١٠﴾

10And do not obey every worthless habitual swearer

Suggest a correction

هَمَّازٍ مَّشَّآءٍۭ بِنَمِيمٍ ﴿١١﴾

11[And] scorner, going about with malicious gossip -

Suggest a correction

مَّنَّاعٍ لِّلْخَيْرِ مُعْتَدٍ أَثِيمٍ ﴿١٢﴾

12A preventer of good, transgressing and sinful,

Suggest a correction

عُتُلٍّۭ بَعْدَ ذَٰلِكَ زَنِيمٍ ﴿١٣﴾

13Cruel, moreover, and an illegitimate pretender.[1725]

[1725] i.e., claiming a particular lineage falsely. The description given in these verses is of al-Waleed bin al-Mugheerah (see also 74:11-25) or possibly, as asserted by Ibn Katheer, al-Akhnas bin Shurayq.
Suggest a correction

أَن كَانَ ذَا مَالٍ وَبَنِينَ ﴿١٤﴾

14Because he is a possessor of wealth and children,

Suggest a correction

إِذَا تُتْلَىٰ عَلَيْهِ ءَايَـٰتُنَا قَالَ أَسَـٰطِيرُ ٱلْأَوَّلِينَ ﴿١٥﴾

15When Our verses are recited to him, he says, "Legends of the former peoples."

Suggest a correction

سَنَسِمُهُۥ عَلَى ٱلْخُرْطُومِ ﴿١٦﴾

16We will brand him upon the snout.[1726]

[1726] Literally, "trunk," meaning the nose of an elephant or pig.
Suggest a correction

إِنَّا بَلَوْنَـٰهُمْ كَمَا بَلَوْنَآ أَصْحَـٰبَ ٱلْجَنَّةِ إِذْ أَقْسَمُوا۟ لَيَصْرِمُنَّهَا مُصْبِحِينَ ﴿١٧﴾

17Indeed, We have tried them as We tried the companions of the garden, when they swore to cut its fruit in the [early] morning

Suggest a correction

وَلَا يَسْتَثْنُونَ ﴿١٨﴾

18Without making exception.[1727]

[1727] i.e., without conceding that nothing can be accomplished unless Allāh wills, saying, "...if Allāh wills" ("in-shā’-Allāh"). See 18:23-24.
Suggest a correction

فَطَافَ عَلَيْهَا طَآئِفٌ مِّن رَّبِّكَ وَهُمْ نَآئِمُونَ ﴿١٩﴾

19So there came upon it [i.e., the garden] an affliction from your Lord while they were asleep.

Suggest a correction

فَأَصْبَحَتْ كَٱلصَّرِيمِ ﴿٢٠﴾

20And it became as though reaped.

Suggest a correction

فَتَنَادَوْا۟ مُصْبِحِينَ ﴿٢١﴾

21And they called one another at morning,

Suggest a correction

أَنِ ٱغْدُوا۟ عَلَىٰ حَرْثِكُمْ إِن كُنتُمْ صَـٰرِمِينَ ﴿٢٢﴾

22[Saying], "Go early to your crop if you would cut the fruit."

Suggest a correction

فَٱنطَلَقُوا۟ وَهُمْ يَتَخَـٰفَتُونَ ﴿٢٣﴾

23So they set out, while lowering their voices,

Suggest a correction

أَن لَّا يَدْخُلَنَّهَا ٱلْيَوْمَ عَلَيْكُم مِّسْكِينٌ ﴿٢٤﴾

24[Saying], "There will surely not enter it today upon you [any] poor person."

Suggest a correction

وَغَدَوْا۟ عَلَىٰ حَرْدٍ قَـٰدِرِينَ ﴿٢٥﴾

25And they went early in determination, [assuming themselves] able.[1728]

[1728] To carry out their plan, confident of their ability.
Suggest a correction

فَلَمَّا رَأَوْهَا قَالُوٓا۟ إِنَّا لَضَآلُّونَ ﴿٢٦﴾

26But when they saw it, they said, "Indeed, we are lost;

Suggest a correction

بَلْ نَحْنُ مَحْرُومُونَ ﴿٢٧﴾

27Rather, we have been deprived."

Suggest a correction

قَالَ أَوْسَطُهُمْ أَلَمْ أَقُل لَّكُمْ لَوْلَا تُسَبِّحُونَ ﴿٢٨﴾

28The most moderate of them said, "Did I not say to you, 'Why do you not exalt [Allāh]?'"[1729]

[1729] i.e., remember or mention Him by saying, "...if Allāh wills." An additional meaning is "praise" or "thank" Him for His bounty.
Suggest a correction

قَالُوا۟ سُبْحَـٰنَ رَبِّنَآ إِنَّا كُنَّا ظَـٰلِمِينَ ﴿٢٩﴾

29They said, "Exalted is our Lord! Indeed, we were wrongdoers."

Suggest a correction

فَأَقْبَلَ بَعْضُهُمْ عَلَىٰ بَعْضٍ يَتَلَـٰوَمُونَ ﴿٣٠﴾

30Then they approached one another, blaming each other.

Suggest a correction

قَالُوا۟ يَـٰوَيْلَنَآ إِنَّا كُنَّا طَـٰغِينَ ﴿٣١﴾

31They said, "O woe to us; indeed we were transgressors.

Suggest a correction

عَسَىٰ رَبُّنَآ أَن يُبْدِلَنَا خَيْرًا مِّنْهَآ إِنَّآ إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا رَٰغِبُونَ ﴿٣٢﴾

32Perhaps our Lord will substitute for us [one] better than it. Indeed, we are toward our Lord desirous."[1730]

[1730] Of His mercy, forgiveness and bounty.
Suggest a correction

كَذَٰلِكَ ٱلْعَذَابُ ۖ وَلَعَذَابُ ٱلْـَٔاخِرَةِ أَكْبَرُ ۚ لَوْ كَانُوا۟ يَعْلَمُونَ ﴿٣٣﴾

33Such is the punishment [of this world]. And the punishment of the Hereafter is greater, if they only knew.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّ لِلْمُتَّقِينَ عِندَ رَبِّهِمْ جَنَّـٰتِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ ﴿٣٤﴾

34Indeed, for the righteous with their Lord are the Gardens of Pleasure.

Suggest a correction

أَفَنَجْعَلُ ٱلْمُسْلِمِينَ كَٱلْمُجْرِمِينَ ﴿٣٥﴾

35Then will We treat the Muslims like the criminals?

Suggest a correction

مَا لَكُمْ كَيْفَ تَحْكُمُونَ ﴿٣٦﴾

36What is [the matter] with you? How do you judge?

Suggest a correction

أَمْ لَكُمْ كِتَـٰبٌ فِيهِ تَدْرُسُونَ ﴿٣٧﴾

37Or do you have a scripture in which you learn

Suggest a correction

إِنَّ لَكُمْ فِيهِ لَمَا تَخَيَّرُونَ ﴿٣٨﴾

38That indeed for you is whatever you choose?

Suggest a correction

أَمْ لَكُمْ أَيْمَـٰنٌ عَلَيْنَا بَـٰلِغَةٌ إِلَىٰ يَوْمِ ٱلْقِيَـٰمَةِ ۙ إِنَّ لَكُمْ لَمَا تَحْكُمُونَ ﴿٣٩﴾

39Or do you have oaths [binding] upon Us, extending until the Day of Resurrection, that indeed for you is whatever you judge?

Suggest a correction

سَلْهُمْ أَيُّهُم بِذَٰلِكَ زَعِيمٌ ﴿٤٠﴾

40Ask them which of them, for that [claim], is responsible.

Suggest a correction

أَمْ لَهُمْ شُرَكَآءُ فَلْيَأْتُوا۟ بِشُرَكَآئِهِمْ إِن كَانُوا۟ صَـٰدِقِينَ ﴿٤١﴾

41Or do they have partners?[1731] Then let them bring their partners, if they should be truthful.

[1731] i.e., those to whom they attribute divinity other than Allāh or partners from among themselves.
Suggest a correction

يَوْمَ يُكْشَفُ عَن سَاقٍ وَيُدْعَوْنَ إِلَى ٱلسُّجُودِ فَلَا يَسْتَطِيعُونَ ﴿٤٢﴾

42The Day the shin will be uncovered[1732] and they are invited to prostration but they [i.e., the disbelievers] will not be able,

[1732] i.e., when everyone will find before him great difficulty. In accordance with authentic ḥadīths, "the shin" might also refer to that of Allāh (subḥānahu wa taʿālā), before which every believer will prostrate on the Day of Judgement. See footnote to 2:19.
Cited in Dost Arabî 2
  • As Colour Soaks into Dust — and the Difference Between a Form and a Form's Existence Fusus · direct citation
  • Fate — What You Said About Yourself in Eternity Fusus · direct citation
Suggest a correction

خَـٰشِعَةً أَبْصَـٰرُهُمْ تَرْهَقُهُمْ ذِلَّةٌ ۖ وَقَدْ كَانُوا۟ يُدْعَوْنَ إِلَى ٱلسُّجُودِ وَهُمْ سَـٰلِمُونَ ﴿٤٣﴾

43Their eyes humbled, humiliation will cover them. And they used to be invited to prostration while they were sound.[1733]

[1733] During worldly life.
Suggest a correction

فَذَرْنِى وَمَن يُكَذِّبُ بِهَـٰذَا ٱلْحَدِيثِ ۖ سَنَسْتَدْرِجُهُم مِّنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَعْلَمُونَ ﴿٤٤﴾

44So leave Me, [O Muḥammad], with [the matter of] whoever denies this statement [i.e., the Qur’ān]. We will progressively lead them [to punishment] from where they do not know.[1734]

[1734] Allāh will increase His favors to them in this world by way of trial, whereby they will sink deeper into sin and thus into destruction.
Suggest a correction

وَأُمْلِى لَهُمْ ۚ إِنَّ كَيْدِى مَتِينٌ ﴿٤٥﴾

45And I will give them time. Indeed, My plan is firm.

Suggest a correction

أَمْ تَسْـَٔلُهُمْ أَجْرًا فَهُم مِّن مَّغْرَمٍ مُّثْقَلُونَ ﴿٤٦﴾

46Or do you ask of them a payment, so they are by debt burdened down?

Suggest a correction

أَمْ عِندَهُمُ ٱلْغَيْبُ فَهُمْ يَكْتُبُونَ ﴿٤٧﴾

47Or have they [knowledge of] the unseen, so they write [it] down?

Suggest a correction

فَٱصْبِرْ لِحُكْمِ رَبِّكَ وَلَا تَكُن كَصَاحِبِ ٱلْحُوتِ إِذْ نَادَىٰ وَهُوَ مَكْظُومٌ ﴿٤٨﴾

48Then be patient for the decision of your Lord, [O Muḥammad], and be not like the companion of the fish [i.e., Jonah] when he called out while he was distressed.

Suggest a correction

لَّوْلَآ أَن تَدَٰرَكَهُۥ نِعْمَةٌ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦ لَنُبِذَ بِٱلْعَرَآءِ وَهُوَ مَذْمُومٌ ﴿٤٩﴾

49If not that a favor [i.e., mercy] from his Lord overtook him, he would have been thrown onto the naked shore while he was censured.[1735]

[1735] But instead, Allāh accepted his repentance and provided means for his recovery. See 37:139-148.
Suggest a correction

فَٱجْتَبَـٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ فَجَعَلَهُۥ مِنَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ ﴿٥٠﴾

50And his Lord chose him and made him of the righteous.

Suggest a correction

وَإِن يَكَادُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ لَيُزْلِقُونَكَ بِأَبْصَـٰرِهِمْ لَمَّا سَمِعُوا۟ ٱلذِّكْرَ وَيَقُولُونَ إِنَّهُۥ لَمَجْنُونٌ ﴿٥١﴾

51And indeed, those who disbelieve would almost make you slip with their eyes [i.e., looks] when they hear the message, and they say, "Indeed, he is mad."

Suggest a correction

وَمَا هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكْرٌ لِّلْعَـٰلَمِينَ ﴿٥٢﴾

52But it is not except a reminder to the worlds.

Suggest a correction
