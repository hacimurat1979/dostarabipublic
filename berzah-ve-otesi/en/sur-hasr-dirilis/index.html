Where the words appear

Each dot is a part or a chapter; its size shows how many times the word appears in that text. Press a dot to open that part or chapter. The strip shows what recurs, not what it means. The counts are an APPROXIMATE scan: Turkish words are searched in the summary texts; a word may also occur in another sense, and some inflected forms may not be counted.
