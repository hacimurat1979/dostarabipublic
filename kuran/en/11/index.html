بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

الٓر ۚ كِتَـٰبٌ أُحْكِمَتْ ءَايَـٰتُهُۥ ثُمَّ فُصِّلَتْ مِن لَّدُنْ حَكِيمٍ خَبِيرٍ ﴿١﴾

1Alif, Lām, Rā.[546] [This is] a Book whose verses are perfected and then presented in detail from [one who is] Wise and Aware[547]

[546] See footnote to 2:1. [547] Refer to footnotes of 6:18.

[555] Referring to Prophet Muḥammad (ﷺ) or to the angel Gabriel. [556] Testifying to its truth. Additionally, it can mean "recites it," i.e., the Qur’ān.
