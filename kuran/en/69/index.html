69 The Reality

Meccan · 52 verses · juz 29 · 2 ruku · revelation order 78

The Arabic text comes from Tanzil and the translations from QuranEnc, unchanged. A translation is not the Qur'an itself; the Arabic text is a separate block. This page is not a commentary or interpretation. Translation shown: Uluslararası Nur Merkezi

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

ٱلْحَآقَّةُ ﴿١﴾

1The Inevitable Reality -

Suggest a correction

مَا ٱلْحَآقَّةُ ﴿٢﴾

2What is the Inevitable Reality?

Suggest a correction

وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلْحَآقَّةُ ﴿٣﴾

3And what can make you know what is the Inevitable Reality?

Suggest a correction

كَذَّبَتْ ثَمُودُ وَعَادٌۢ بِٱلْقَارِعَةِ ﴿٤﴾

4Thamūd and ʿAad denied the Striking Calamity [i.e., the Resurrection].

Suggest a correction

فَأَمَّا ثَمُودُ فَأُهْلِكُوا۟ بِٱلطَّاغِيَةِ ﴿٥﴾

5So as for Thamūd, they were destroyed by the overpowering [blast].

Suggest a correction

وَأَمَّا عَادٌ فَأُهْلِكُوا۟ بِرِيحٍ صَرْصَرٍ عَاتِيَةٍ ﴿٦﴾

6And as for ʿAad, they were destroyed by a screaming,[1737] violent wind

[1737] Or "cold."
Suggest a correction

سَخَّرَهَا عَلَيْهِمْ سَبْعَ لَيَالٍ وَثَمَـٰنِيَةَ أَيَّامٍ حُسُومًا فَتَرَى ٱلْقَوْمَ فِيهَا صَرْعَىٰ كَأَنَّهُمْ أَعْجَازُ نَخْلٍ خَاوِيَةٍ ﴿٧﴾

7Which He [i.e., Allāh] imposed upon them for seven nights and eight days in succession, so you would see the people therein fallen as if they were hollow trunks of palm trees.

Suggest a correction

فَهَلْ تَرَىٰ لَهُم مِّنۢ بَاقِيَةٍ ﴿٨﴾

8Then do you see of them any remains?

Suggest a correction

وَجَآءَ فِرْعَوْنُ وَمَن قَبْلَهُۥ وَٱلْمُؤْتَفِكَـٰتُ بِٱلْخَاطِئَةِ ﴿٩﴾

9And there came Pharaoh and those before him and the overturned cities[1738] with sin.

[1738] Those to which Lot was sent (see 11:82-83) or generally, all cities which were destroyed due to their denial of a messenger from Allāh.
Suggest a correction

فَعَصَوْا۟ رَسُولَ رَبِّهِمْ فَأَخَذَهُمْ أَخْذَةً رَّابِيَةً ﴿١٠﴾

10And they disobeyed the messenger of their Lord, so He seized them with a seizure exceeding [in severity].

Suggest a correction

إِنَّا لَمَّا طَغَا ٱلْمَآءُ حَمَلْنَـٰكُمْ فِى ٱلْجَارِيَةِ ﴿١١﴾

11Indeed, when the water overflowed, We carried you [i.e., your ancestors] in the sailing ship[1739]

[1739] Which was constructed by Noah.
Suggest a correction

لِنَجْعَلَهَا لَكُمْ تَذْكِرَةً وَتَعِيَهَآ أُذُنٌ وَٰعِيَةٌ ﴿١٢﴾

12That We might make it for you a reminder and [that] a conscious ear would be conscious of it.

Suggest a correction

فَإِذَا نُفِخَ فِى ٱلصُّورِ نَفْخَةٌ وَٰحِدَةٌ ﴿١٣﴾

13Then when the Horn is blown with one blast

Suggest a correction

وَحُمِلَتِ ٱلْأَرْضُ وَٱلْجِبَالُ فَدُكَّتَا دَكَّةً وَٰحِدَةً ﴿١٤﴾

14And the earth and the mountains are lifted and leveled with one blow [i.e., stroke] -

Suggest a correction

فَيَوْمَئِذٍ وَقَعَتِ ٱلْوَاقِعَةُ ﴿١٥﴾

15Then on that Day, the Occurrence [i.e., Resurrection] will occur,

Suggest a correction

وَٱنشَقَّتِ ٱلسَّمَآءُ فَهِىَ يَوْمَئِذٍ وَاهِيَةٌ ﴿١٦﴾

16And the heaven will split [open], for that Day it is infirm.[1740]

[1740] i.e., weak, enfeebled and unstable.
Suggest a correction

وَٱلْمَلَكُ عَلَىٰٓ أَرْجَآئِهَا ۚ وَيَحْمِلُ عَرْشَ رَبِّكَ فَوْقَهُمْ يَوْمَئِذٍ ثَمَـٰنِيَةٌ ﴿١٧﴾

17And the angels are at its edges. And there will bear the Throne of your Lord above them, that Day, eight [of them].

Suggest a correction

يَوْمَئِذٍ تُعْرَضُونَ لَا تَخْفَىٰ مِنكُمْ خَافِيَةٌ ﴿١٨﴾

18That Day, you will be exhibited [for judgement]; not hidden among you is anything concealed.[1741]

[1741] i.e., any person or any secret you might attempt to conceal.
Suggest a correction

فَأَمَّا مَنْ أُوتِىَ كِتَـٰبَهُۥ بِيَمِينِهِۦ فَيَقُولُ هَآؤُمُ ٱقْرَءُوا۟ كِتَـٰبِيَهْ ﴿١٩﴾

19So as for he who is given his record in his right hand, he will say, "Here, read my record!

Suggest a correction

إِنِّى ظَنَنتُ أَنِّى مُلَـٰقٍ حِسَابِيَهْ ﴿٢٠﴾

20Indeed, I was certain that I would be meeting my account."

Suggest a correction

فَهُوَ فِى عِيشَةٍ رَّاضِيَةٍ ﴿٢١﴾

21So he will be in a pleasant life -

Suggest a correction

فِى جَنَّةٍ عَالِيَةٍ ﴿٢٢﴾

22In an elevated garden,

Suggest a correction

قُطُوفُهَا دَانِيَةٌ ﴿٢٣﴾

23Its [fruit] to be picked hanging near.

Suggest a correction

كُلُوا۟ وَٱشْرَبُوا۟ هَنِيٓـًٔۢا بِمَآ أَسْلَفْتُمْ فِى ٱلْأَيَّامِ ٱلْخَالِيَةِ ﴿٢٤﴾

24[They will be told], "Eat and drink in satisfaction for what you put forth[1742] in the days past."

[1742] Literally, "advanced" in anticipation of reward in the Hereafter.
Suggest a correction

وَأَمَّا مَنْ أُوتِىَ كِتَـٰبَهُۥ بِشِمَالِهِۦ فَيَقُولُ يَـٰلَيْتَنِى لَمْ أُوتَ كِتَـٰبِيَهْ ﴿٢٥﴾

25But as for he who is given his record in his left hand, he will say, "Oh, I wish I had not been given my record

Suggest a correction

وَلَمْ أَدْرِ مَا حِسَابِيَهْ ﴿٢٦﴾

26And had not known what is my account.

Suggest a correction

يَـٰلَيْتَهَا كَانَتِ ٱلْقَاضِيَةَ ﴿٢٧﴾

27I wish it [i.e., my death] had been the decisive one.[1743]

[1743] i.e., ending life rather than being the gateway to eternal life.
Suggest a correction

مَآ أَغْنَىٰ عَنِّى مَالِيَهْ ۜ ﴿٢٨﴾

28My wealth has not availed me.

Suggest a correction

هَلَكَ عَنِّى سُلْطَـٰنِيَهْ ﴿٢٩﴾

29Gone from me is my authority."[1744]

[1744] i.e., I have no valid excuse to stand on.
Suggest a correction

خُذُوهُ فَغُلُّوهُ ﴿٣٠﴾

30[Allāh will say], "Seize him and shackle him.

Suggest a correction

ثُمَّ ٱلْجَحِيمَ صَلُّوهُ ﴿٣١﴾

31Then into Hellfire drive him.

Suggest a correction

ثُمَّ فِى سِلْسِلَةٍ ذَرْعُهَا سَبْعُونَ ذِرَاعًا فَٱسْلُكُوهُ ﴿٣٢﴾

32Then into a chain whose length is seventy cubits insert him."

Suggest a correction

إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ لَا يُؤْمِنُ بِٱللَّهِ ٱلْعَظِيمِ ﴿٣٣﴾

33Indeed, he did not used to believe in Allāh, the Most Great,

Suggest a correction

وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ ٱلْمِسْكِينِ ﴿٣٤﴾

34Nor did he encourage the feeding of the poor.

Suggest a correction

فَلَيْسَ لَهُ ٱلْيَوْمَ هَـٰهُنَا حَمِيمٌ ﴿٣٥﴾

35So there is not for him here this Day any devoted friend

Suggest a correction

وَلَا طَعَامٌ إِلَّا مِنْ غِسْلِينٍ ﴿٣٦﴾

36Nor any food except from the discharge of wounds;

Suggest a correction

لَّا يَأْكُلُهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱلْخَـٰطِـُٔونَ ﴿٣٧﴾

37None will eat it except the sinners.

Suggest a correction

فَلَآ أُقْسِمُ بِمَا تُبْصِرُونَ ﴿٣٨﴾

38So I swear by what you see

Suggest a correction

وَمَا لَا تُبْصِرُونَ ﴿٣٩﴾

39And what you do not see

Suggest a correction

إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوْلُ رَسُولٍ كَرِيمٍ ﴿٤٠﴾

40[That] indeed, it [i.e., the Qur’ān] is the word of a noble Messenger.

Suggest a correction

وَمَا هُوَ بِقَوْلِ شَاعِرٍ ۚ قَلِيلًا مَّا تُؤْمِنُونَ ﴿٤١﴾

41And it is not the word of a poet; little do you believe.

Suggest a correction

وَلَا بِقَوْلِ كَاهِنٍ ۚ قَلِيلًا مَّا تَذَكَّرُونَ ﴿٤٢﴾

42Nor the word of a soothsayer; little do you remember.

Suggest a correction

تَنزِيلٌ مِّن رَّبِّ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ ﴿٤٣﴾

43[It is] a revelation from the Lord of the worlds.

Suggest a correction

وَلَوْ تَقَوَّلَ عَلَيْنَا بَعْضَ ٱلْأَقَاوِيلِ ﴿٤٤﴾

44And if he [i.e., Muḥammad] had made up about Us some [false] sayings,

Suggest a correction

لَأَخَذْنَا مِنْهُ بِٱلْيَمِينِ ﴿٤٥﴾

45We would have seized him by the right hand;[1745]

[1745] Another interpretation is "by [Our] right hand," i.e., Allāh would have exacted revenge with might and power.
Suggest a correction

ثُمَّ لَقَطَعْنَا مِنْهُ ٱلْوَتِينَ ﴿٤٦﴾

46Then We would have cut from him the aorta.[1746]

[1746] Causing immediate death.
Suggest a correction

فَمَا مِنكُم مِّنْ أَحَدٍ عَنْهُ حَـٰجِزِينَ ﴿٤٧﴾

47And there is no one of you who could prevent [Us] from him.

Suggest a correction

وَإِنَّهُۥ لَتَذْكِرَةٌ لِّلْمُتَّقِينَ ﴿٤٨﴾

48And indeed, it [i.e., the Qur’ān] is a reminder for the righteous.

Suggest a correction

وَإِنَّا لَنَعْلَمُ أَنَّ مِنكُم مُّكَذِّبِينَ ﴿٤٩﴾

49And indeed, We know that among you are deniers.

Suggest a correction

وَإِنَّهُۥ لَحَسْرَةٌ عَلَى ٱلْكَـٰفِرِينَ ﴿٥٠﴾

50And indeed, it will be [a cause of] regret upon the disbelievers.

Suggest a correction

وَإِنَّهُۥ لَحَقُّ ٱلْيَقِينِ ﴿٥١﴾

51And indeed, it is the truth of certainty.

Suggest a correction

فَسَبِّحْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلْعَظِيمِ ﴿٥٢﴾

52So exalt the name of your Lord, the Most Great.

Suggest a correction
