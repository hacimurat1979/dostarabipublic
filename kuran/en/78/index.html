78 The Tidings

Meccan · 40 verses · juz 30 · 2 ruku · revelation order 80

The Arabic text comes from Tanzil and the translations from QuranEnc, unchanged. A translation is not the Qur'an itself; the Arabic text is a separate block. This page is not a commentary or interpretation. Translation shown: Uluslararası Nur Merkezi

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

۞عَمَّ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ ﴿١﴾

1About what are they asking one another?

Suggest a correction

عَنِ ٱلنَّبَإِ ٱلْعَظِيمِ ﴿٢﴾

2About the great news[1821] -

[1821] i.e., the Resurrection.
Suggest a correction

ٱلَّذِى هُمْ فِيهِ مُخْتَلِفُونَ ﴿٣﴾

3That over which they are in disagreement.

Suggest a correction

كَلَّا سَيَعْلَمُونَ ﴿٤﴾

4No! They are going to know.

Suggest a correction

ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَيَعْلَمُونَ ﴿٥﴾

5Then, no! They are going to know.

Suggest a correction

أَلَمْ نَجْعَلِ ٱلْأَرْضَ مِهَـٰدًا ﴿٦﴾

6Have We not made the earth a resting place?

Suggest a correction

وَٱلْجِبَالَ أَوْتَادًا ﴿٧﴾

7And the mountains as stakes?[1822]

[1822] To stabilize the land and balance the earth.
Suggest a correction

وَخَلَقْنَـٰكُمْ أَزْوَٰجًا ﴿٨﴾

8And We created you in pairs

Suggest a correction

وَجَعَلْنَا نَوْمَكُمْ سُبَاتًا ﴿٩﴾

9And made your sleep [a means for] rest

Suggest a correction

وَجَعَلْنَا ٱلَّيْلَ لِبَاسًا ﴿١٠﴾

10And made the night as clothing[1823]

[1823] Covering you in darkness and providing rest.
Suggest a correction

وَجَعَلْنَا ٱلنَّهَارَ مَعَاشًا ﴿١١﴾

11And made the day for livelihood

Suggest a correction

وَبَنَيْنَا فَوْقَكُمْ سَبْعًا شِدَادًا ﴿١٢﴾

12And constructed above you seven strong [heavens]

Suggest a correction

وَجَعَلْنَا سِرَاجًا وَهَّاجًا ﴿١٣﴾

13And made [therein] a burning lamp

Suggest a correction

وَأَنزَلْنَا مِنَ ٱلْمُعْصِرَٰتِ مَآءً ثَجَّاجًا ﴿١٤﴾

14And sent down from the rain clouds pouring water

Suggest a correction

لِّنُخْرِجَ بِهِۦ حَبًّا وَنَبَاتًا ﴿١٥﴾

15That We may bring forth thereby grain and vegetation

Suggest a correction

وَجَنَّـٰتٍ أَلْفَافًا ﴿١٦﴾

16And gardens of entwined growth.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّ يَوْمَ ٱلْفَصْلِ كَانَ مِيقَـٰتًا ﴿١٧﴾

17Indeed, the Day of Judgement is an appointed time -

Suggest a correction

يَوْمَ يُنفَخُ فِى ٱلصُّورِ فَتَأْتُونَ أَفْوَاجًا ﴿١٨﴾

18The Day the Horn is blown and you will come forth in multitudes

Suggest a correction

وَفُتِحَتِ ٱلسَّمَآءُ فَكَانَتْ أَبْوَٰبًا ﴿١٩﴾

19And the heaven is opened and will become gateways

Suggest a correction

وَسُيِّرَتِ ٱلْجِبَالُ فَكَانَتْ سَرَابًا ﴿٢٠﴾

20And the mountains are removed and will be [but] a mirage.

Suggest a correction

إِنَّ جَهَنَّمَ كَانَتْ مِرْصَادًا ﴿٢١﴾

21Indeed, Hell has been lying in wait

Suggest a correction

لِّلطَّـٰغِينَ مَـَٔابًا ﴿٢٢﴾

22For the transgressors, a place of return,

Suggest a correction

لَّـٰبِثِينَ فِيهَآ أَحْقَابًا ﴿٢٣﴾

23In which they will remain for ages [unending].

Cited in Dost Arabî 2
  • Everyone Walks Straight on His Own Path — and Still There Is a Call Fusus · direct citation
  • Bringing-to-Be Takes Three — and There Are Three on the Other Side Fusus · direct citation
Suggest a correction

لَّا يَذُوقُونَ فِيهَا بَرْدًا وَلَا شَرَابًا ﴿٢٤﴾

24They will not taste therein [any] coolness or drink.

Suggest a correction

إِلَّا حَمِيمًا وَغَسَّاقًا ﴿٢٥﴾

25Except scalding water and [foul] purulence -

Suggest a correction

جَزَآءً وِفَاقًا ﴿٢٦﴾

26An appropriate recompense.[1824]

[1824] In proportion to and comparable with their crimes.
Suggest a correction

إِنَّهُمْ كَانُوا۟ لَا يَرْجُونَ حِسَابًا ﴿٢٧﴾

27Indeed, they were not expecting an account

Suggest a correction

وَكَذَّبُوا۟ بِـَٔايَـٰتِنَا كِذَّابًا ﴿٢٨﴾

28And denied Our verses with [emphatic] denial.

Suggest a correction

وَكُلَّ شَىْءٍ أَحْصَيْنَـٰهُ كِتَـٰبًا ﴿٢٩﴾

29But all things We have enumerated in writing.

Suggest a correction

فَذُوقُوا۟ فَلَن نَّزِيدَكُمْ إِلَّا عَذَابًا ﴿٣٠﴾

30"So taste [the penalty], and never will We increase you except in torment."[1825]

[1825] This announcement will be made to the companions of Hell.
Suggest a correction

إِنَّ لِلْمُتَّقِينَ مَفَازًا ﴿٣١﴾

31Indeed, for the righteous is attainment[1826] -

[1826] Of security, success and reward, including escape and safety from Hell.
Suggest a correction

حَدَآئِقَ وَأَعْنَـٰبًا ﴿٣٢﴾

32Gardens and grapevines

Suggest a correction

وَكَوَاعِبَ أَتْرَابًا ﴿٣٣﴾

33And young women [companions] of equal age.

Suggest a correction

وَكَأْسًا دِهَاقًا ﴿٣٤﴾

34And a full cup.[1827]

[1827] Of wine which is delicious and does not intoxicate.
Suggest a correction

لَّا يَسْمَعُونَ فِيهَا لَغْوًا وَلَا كِذَّٰبًا ﴿٣٥﴾

35No ill speech will they hear therein or any falsehood -

Suggest a correction

جَزَآءً مِّن رَّبِّكَ عَطَآءً حِسَابًا ﴿٣٦﴾

36[As] reward from your Lord, [a generous] gift [made due by] account,[1828]

[1828] i.e., as a result of both their own righteous deeds and the limitless generosity of Allāh (subḥānahu wa taʿālā) . Another meaning is "a gift calculated [to be adequate]."
Suggest a correction

رَّبِّ ٱلسَّمَـٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ وَمَا بَيْنَهُمَا ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ۖ لَا يَمْلِكُونَ مِنْهُ خِطَابًا ﴿٣٧﴾

37[From] the Lord of the heavens and the earth and whatever is between them, the Most Merciful. They possess not from Him [authority for] speech.[1829]

[1829] None of Allāh's creatures can plead with Him on the Day of Judgement except by His permission.
Suggest a correction

يَوْمَ يَقُومُ ٱلرُّوحُ وَٱلْمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ صَفًّا ۖ لَّا يَتَكَلَّمُونَ إِلَّا مَنْ أَذِنَ لَهُ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنُ وَقَالَ صَوَابًا ﴿٣٨﴾

38The Day that the Spirit [i.e., Gabriel] and the angels will stand in rows, they will not speak except for one whom the Most Merciful permits, and he will say what is correct.

Suggest a correction

ذَٰلِكَ ٱلْيَوْمُ ٱلْحَقُّ ۖ فَمَن شَآءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ مَـَٔابًا ﴿٣٩﴾

39That is the True [i.e., certain] Day; so he who wills may take to his Lord a [way of] return.[1830]

[1830] i.e., a direct route through correct beliefs and righteous deeds.
Suggest a correction

إِنَّآ أَنذَرْنَـٰكُمْ عَذَابًا قَرِيبًا يَوْمَ يَنظُرُ ٱلْمَرْءُ مَا قَدَّمَتْ يَدَاهُ وَيَقُولُ ٱلْكَافِرُ يَـٰلَيْتَنِى كُنتُ تُرَٰبًۢا ﴿٤٠﴾

40Indeed, We have warned you of an impending punishment on the Day when a man will observe what his hands have put forth[1831] and the disbeliever will say, "Oh, I wish that I were dust!"

[1831] i.e., the deeds he did in this world, which await him in the Hereafter.
Suggest a correction
