50 The Letter "Qaf"

Meccan · 45 verses · juz 26 · 3 ruku · revelation order 34

The Arabic text comes from Tanzil and the translations from QuranEnc, unchanged. A translation is not the Qur'an itself; the Arabic text is a separate block. This page is not a commentary or interpretation. Translation shown: Uluslararası Nur Merkezi

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

قٓ ۚ وَٱلْقُرْءَانِ ٱلْمَجِيدِ ﴿١﴾

1Qāf.[1524] By the honored Qur’ān...[1525]

[1524] See footnote to 2:1. [1525] See footnote to 38:1.
Suggest a correction

بَلْ عَجِبُوٓا۟ أَن جَآءَهُم مُّنذِرٌ مِّنْهُمْ فَقَالَ ٱلْكَـٰفِرُونَ هَـٰذَا شَىْءٌ عَجِيبٌ ﴿٢﴾

2But they wonder that there has come to them a warner from among themselves, and the disbelievers say, "This is an amazing thing.

Suggest a correction

أَءِذَا مِتْنَا وَكُنَّا تُرَابًا ۖ ذَٰلِكَ رَجْعٌۢ بَعِيدٌ ﴿٣﴾

3When we have died and have become dust, [we will return to life]? That is a distant [i.e., unlikely] return."

Suggest a correction

قَدْ عَلِمْنَا مَا تَنقُصُ ٱلْأَرْضُ مِنْهُمْ ۖ وَعِندَنَا كِتَـٰبٌ حَفِيظٌۢ ﴿٤﴾

4We know what the earth diminishes [i.e., consumes] of them, and with Us is a retaining record.

Suggest a correction

بَلْ كَذَّبُوا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ لَمَّا جَآءَهُمْ فَهُمْ فِىٓ أَمْرٍ مَّرِيجٍ ﴿٥﴾

5But they denied the truth when it came to them, so they are in a confused condition.

Suggest a correction

أَفَلَمْ يَنظُرُوٓا۟ إِلَى ٱلسَّمَآءِ فَوْقَهُمْ كَيْفَ بَنَيْنَـٰهَا وَزَيَّنَّـٰهَا وَمَا لَهَا مِن فُرُوجٍ ﴿٦﴾

6Have they not looked at the heaven above them - how We structured it and adorned it and [how] it has no rifts?

Suggest a correction

وَٱلْأَرْضَ مَدَدْنَـٰهَا وَأَلْقَيْنَا فِيهَا رَوَٰسِىَ وَأَنۢبَتْنَا فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ زَوْجٍۭ بَهِيجٍ ﴿٧﴾

7And the earth - We spread it out and cast therein firmly set mountains and made grow therein [something] of every beautiful kind,

Suggest a correction

تَبْصِرَةً وَذِكْرَىٰ لِكُلِّ عَبْدٍ مُّنِيبٍ ﴿٨﴾

8Giving insight and a reminder for every servant who turns [to Allāh].

Suggest a correction

وَنَزَّلْنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءً مُّبَـٰرَكًا فَأَنۢبَتْنَا بِهِۦ جَنَّـٰتٍ وَحَبَّ ٱلْحَصِيدِ ﴿٩﴾

9And We have sent down blessed rain from the sky and made grow thereby gardens and grain from the harvest

Suggest a correction

وَٱلنَّخْلَ بَاسِقَـٰتٍ لَّهَا طَلْعٌ نَّضِيدٌ ﴿١٠﴾

10And lofty palm trees having fruit arranged in layers -

Suggest a correction

رِّزْقًا لِّلْعِبَادِ ۖ وَأَحْيَيْنَا بِهِۦ بَلْدَةً مَّيْتًا ۚ كَذَٰلِكَ ٱلْخُرُوجُ ﴿١١﴾

11As provision for the servants, and We have given life thereby to a dead land. Thus is the emergence [i.e., resurrection].

Suggest a correction

كَذَّبَتْ قَبْلَهُمْ قَوْمُ نُوحٍ وَأَصْحَـٰبُ ٱلرَّسِّ وَثَمُودُ ﴿١٢﴾

12The people of Noah denied before them,[1526] and the companions of the well[1527] and Thamūd

[1526] i.e., before the disbelievers of Makkah. [1527] See footnote to 25:38.
Suggest a correction

وَعَادٌ وَفِرْعَوْنُ وَإِخْوَٰنُ لُوطٍ ﴿١٣﴾

13And ʿAad and Pharaoh and the brothers [i.e., people] of Lot

Suggest a correction

وَأَصْحَـٰبُ ٱلْأَيْكَةِ وَقَوْمُ تُبَّعٍ ۚ كُلٌّ كَذَّبَ ٱلرُّسُلَ فَحَقَّ وَعِيدِ ﴿١٤﴾

14And the companions of the thicket and the people of Tubbaʿ. All denied the messengers, so My threat was justly fulfilled.

Suggest a correction

أَفَعَيِينَا بِٱلْخَلْقِ ٱلْأَوَّلِ ۚ بَلْ هُمْ فِى لَبْسٍ مِّنْ خَلْقٍ جَدِيدٍ ﴿١٥﴾

15Did We fail in the first creation? But they are in confusion over a new creation.

Suggest a correction

وَلَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ وَنَعْلَمُ مَا تُوَسْوِسُ بِهِۦ نَفْسُهُۥ ۖ وَنَحْنُ أَقْرَبُ إِلَيْهِ مِنْ حَبْلِ ٱلْوَرِيدِ ﴿١٦﴾

16And We have already created man and know what his soul whispers to him, and We are closer[1528] to him than [his] jugular vein.

[1528] In absolute knowledge of everything about him. "We" has also been interpreted to mean the angels who are mentioned in the following verse.
Cited in Dost Arabî 1
  • Everyone Walks Straight on His Own Path — and Still There Is a Call Fusus · direct citation
Suggest a correction

إِذْ يَتَلَقَّى ٱلْمُتَلَقِّيَانِ عَنِ ٱلْيَمِينِ وَعَنِ ٱلشِّمَالِ قَعِيدٌ ﴿١٧﴾

17When the two receivers [i.e., recording angels] receive,[1529] seated on the right and on the left.

[1529] And record each word and deed.
Suggest a correction

مَّا يَلْفِظُ مِن قَوْلٍ إِلَّا لَدَيْهِ رَقِيبٌ عَتِيدٌ ﴿١٨﴾

18He [i.e., man] utters no word except that with him is an observer prepared [to record].

Suggest a correction

وَجَآءَتْ سَكْرَةُ ٱلْمَوْتِ بِٱلْحَقِّ ۖ ذَٰلِكَ مَا كُنتَ مِنْهُ تَحِيدُ ﴿١٩﴾

19And the intoxication of death will bring the truth; that is what you were trying to avoid.

Suggest a correction

وَنُفِخَ فِى ٱلصُّورِ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ يَوْمُ ٱلْوَعِيدِ ﴿٢٠﴾

20And the Horn will be blown. That is the Day of [carrying out] the threat.

Suggest a correction

وَجَآءَتْ كُلُّ نَفْسٍ مَّعَهَا سَآئِقٌ وَشَهِيدٌ ﴿٢١﴾

21And every soul will come, with it a driver and a witness.[1530]

[1530] i.e., one angel driving the soul to the Judgement and one to testify as to its deeds.
Suggest a correction

لَّقَدْ كُنتَ فِى غَفْلَةٍ مِّنْ هَـٰذَا فَكَشَفْنَا عَنكَ غِطَآءَكَ فَبَصَرُكَ ٱلْيَوْمَ حَدِيدٌ ﴿٢٢﴾

22[It will be said], "You were certainly in unmindfulness of this, and We have removed from you your cover,[1531] so your sight, this Day, is sharp."

[1531] Of heedlessness, or that which had sealed your hearing, your vision and your heart from guidance.
Suggest a correction

وَقَالَ قَرِينُهُۥ هَـٰذَا مَا لَدَىَّ عَتِيدٌ ﴿٢٣﴾

23And his companion, [the angel], will say, "This [record] is what is with me, prepared."

Suggest a correction

أَلْقِيَا فِى جَهَنَّمَ كُلَّ كَفَّارٍ عَنِيدٍ ﴿٢٤﴾

24[Allāh will say], "Throw into Hell every obstinate disbeliever,

Suggest a correction

مَّنَّاعٍ لِّلْخَيْرِ مُعْتَدٍ مُّرِيبٍ ﴿٢٥﴾

25Preventer of good, aggressor, and doubter,

Suggest a correction

ٱلَّذِى جَعَلَ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَـٰهًا ءَاخَرَ فَأَلْقِيَاهُ فِى ٱلْعَذَابِ ٱلشَّدِيدِ ﴿٢٦﴾

26Who made [as equal] with Allāh another deity; then throw him into the severe punishment."

Suggest a correction

قَالَ قَرِينُهُۥ رَبَّنَا مَآ أَطْغَيْتُهُۥ وَلَـٰكِن كَانَ فِى ضَلَـٰلٍۭ بَعِيدٍ ﴿٢٧﴾

27His [devil] companion will say, "Our Lord, I did not make him transgress, but he [himself] was in extreme error."

Suggest a correction

قَالَ لَا تَخْتَصِمُوا۟ لَدَىَّ وَقَدْ قَدَّمْتُ إِلَيْكُم بِٱلْوَعِيدِ ﴿٢٨﴾

28[Allāh] will say, "Do not dispute before Me, while I had already presented to you the threat [i.e., warning].

Suggest a correction

مَا يُبَدَّلُ ٱلْقَوْلُ لَدَىَّ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ بِظَلَّـٰمٍ لِّلْعَبِيدِ ﴿٢٩﴾

29The word [i.e., decree] will not be changed with Me, and never will I be unjust to the servants."

Cited in Dost Arabî 2
  • Fear the Book, Not Me — But the Book Was Written From Your Own Form Futuhat · direct citation
  • Power Is Incidental — Origin Is Weakness Fusus · direct citation
Suggest a correction

يَوْمَ نَقُولُ لِجَهَنَّمَ هَلِ ٱمْتَلَأْتِ وَتَقُولُ هَلْ مِن مَّزِيدٍ ﴿٣٠﴾

30On the Day We will say to Hell, "Have you been filled?" and it will say, "Are there some more,"

Suggest a correction

وَأُزْلِفَتِ ٱلْجَنَّةُ لِلْمُتَّقِينَ غَيْرَ بَعِيدٍ ﴿٣١﴾

31And Paradise will be brought near to the righteous, not far,

Suggest a correction

هَـٰذَا مَا تُوعَدُونَ لِكُلِّ أَوَّابٍ حَفِيظٍ ﴿٣٢﴾

32[It will be said], "This is what you were promised - for every returner [to Allāh] and keeper [of His covenant].

Suggest a correction

مَّنْ خَشِىَ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنَ بِٱلْغَيْبِ وَجَآءَ بِقَلْبٍ مُّنِيبٍ ﴿٣٣﴾

33Who feared the Most Merciful in the unseen and came with a heart returning [in repentance].

Suggest a correction

ٱدْخُلُوهَا بِسَلَـٰمٍ ۖ ذَٰلِكَ يَوْمُ ٱلْخُلُودِ ﴿٣٤﴾

34Enter it in peace. This is the Day of Eternity."

Suggest a correction

لَهُم مَّا يَشَآءُونَ فِيهَا وَلَدَيْنَا مَزِيدٌ ﴿٣٥﴾

35They will have whatever they wish therein, and with Us is more.

Suggest a correction

وَكَمْ أَهْلَكْنَا قَبْلَهُم مِّن قَرْنٍ هُمْ أَشَدُّ مِنْهُم بَطْشًا فَنَقَّبُوا۟ فِى ٱلْبِلَـٰدِ هَلْ مِن مَّحِيصٍ ﴿٣٦﴾

36And how many a generation before them did We destroy who were greater than them in [striking] power and had explored throughout the lands. Is there any place of escape?

Suggest a correction

إِنَّ فِى ذَٰلِكَ لَذِكْرَىٰ لِمَن كَانَ لَهُۥ قَلْبٌ أَوْ أَلْقَى ٱلسَّمْعَ وَهُوَ شَهِيدٌ ﴿٣٧﴾

37Indeed in that is a reminder for whoever has a heart or who listens while he is present [in mind].

Cited in Dost Arabî 2
  • The Heart Is Named for Its Turning Fusus · direct citation
  • The Word of Muhammad Fusus · direct citation
Suggest a correction

وَلَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا ٱلسَّمَـٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ وَمَا بَيْنَهُمَا فِى سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٍ وَمَا مَسَّنَا مِن لُّغُوبٍ ﴿٣٨﴾

38And We did certainly create the heavens and earth and what is between them in six days, and there touched Us no weariness.

Suggest a correction

فَٱصْبِرْ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ وَسَبِّحْ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ قَبْلَ طُلُوعِ ٱلشَّمْسِ وَقَبْلَ ٱلْغُرُوبِ ﴿٣٩﴾

39So be patient, [O Muḥammad], over what they say and exalt [Allāh] with praise of your Lord before the rising of the sun and before its setting,

Suggest a correction

وَمِنَ ٱلَّيْلِ فَسَبِّحْهُ وَأَدْبَـٰرَ ٱلسُّجُودِ ﴿٤٠﴾

40And [in part] of the night exalt Him and after prostration [i.e., prayer].

Suggest a correction

وَٱسْتَمِعْ يَوْمَ يُنَادِ ٱلْمُنَادِ مِن مَّكَانٍ قَرِيبٍ ﴿٤١﴾

41And listen on the Day when the Caller[1532] will call out from a place that is near -

[1532] An angel who will call out Allāh's command for the Resurrection.
Suggest a correction

يَوْمَ يَسْمَعُونَ ٱلصَّيْحَةَ بِٱلْحَقِّ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ يَوْمُ ٱلْخُرُوجِ ﴿٤٢﴾

42The Day they will hear the blast [of the Horn] in truth. That is the Day of Emergence [from the graves].

Suggest a correction

إِنَّا نَحْنُ نُحْىِۦ وَنُمِيتُ وَإِلَيْنَا ٱلْمَصِيرُ ﴿٤٣﴾

43Indeed, it is We who give life and cause death, and to Us is the destination

Suggest a correction

يَوْمَ تَشَقَّقُ ٱلْأَرْضُ عَنْهُمْ سِرَاعًا ۚ ذَٰلِكَ حَشْرٌ عَلَيْنَا يَسِيرٌ ﴿٤٤﴾

44On the Day the earth breaks away from them [and they emerge] rapidly; that is a gathering easy for Us.

Suggest a correction

نَّحْنُ أَعْلَمُ بِمَا يَقُولُونَ ۖ وَمَآ أَنتَ عَلَيْهِم بِجَبَّارٍ ۖ فَذَكِّرْ بِٱلْقُرْءَانِ مَن يَخَافُ وَعِيدِ ﴿٤٥﴾

45We are most knowing of what they say, and you are not over them a tyrant.[1533] But remind by the Qur’ān whoever fears My threat.

[1533] Forcing people to belief or submission.
